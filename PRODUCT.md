# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Mixed casual + competitive word-game players, ages 15-40. Phones primarily, plus TV/party screens. Five languages including Hebrew (RTL). In Brain Gym specifically: players come to feel their brain getting sharper — short, repeatable cognitive drills (memory, speed, focus, flexibility, vocabulary) they return to daily.

## Product Purpose
LexiClash is a quirky, competitive word game. Brain Gym is its single-player cognitive-training mode: a hub of bite-size drills that score five cognitive domains, track progress over time, and reward improvement. Success = players feel real, legible progress ("I remember one more word than last week") and come back tomorrow.

## Brand Personality
Quirky, electric, loud — party energy + competitive edge + surprising charm. Encouraging coach, never clinical. Celebrates wins big; softens misses.

## Anti-references
Generic mobile-game UI. Soft gradients. Glassmorphism. Corporate/clinical "brain training" aesthetics (Lumosity-blue, sterile). Gradient text. Endless identical card grids. Cluttered screens that force scrolling past the play surface.

## Design Principles
- **Energy with intention** — personality everywhere, but never at the cost of clarity.
- **Phone AND TV** — the play surface (board) is sacred; never squash it, never make players scroll to reach it.
- **Competitive clarity** — progress and score must be instantly legible; insights must be real, not decorative.
- **Coherent chaos** — neo-brutalist: hard pixel shadows, solid borders, electric color-coded modes. Refined, not messy.
- **Celebrate progress** — show the player they're getting better in human terms (words remembered, % memory gain).

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast (body ≥4.5:1) — current known contrast debt to fix. RTL (Hebrew) must mirror correctly. `prefers-reduced-motion` honored on every animation. Color-coded modes must not rely on color alone.
