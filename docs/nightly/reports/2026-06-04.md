- **OP question:** "The sweet spot is when skill matters, but so does adaptability — when the game forces you to think beyond just knowing words."
- **Suggested reply (copy-paste ready):**

For me the killer is when knowing more words just means you never have to adapt — vocabulary as a ceiling, not a tool. The games that stick add some external pressure that makes your second-best word occasionally the right call: shrinking clocks, board states that punish the obvious play, opponents reacting to what you just scored. I've been poking at this with a multiplayer Boggle-variant (lexiclash.live) and the most interesting sessions are when a player with a smaller vocabulary outplays someone who just fires off longer words on autopilot. Curiosity: do people here find that *time pressure* vs *score pressure* hits differently?
