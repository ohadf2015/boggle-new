#### Top Reddit pick of the day
- **Thread:** https://www.reddit.com/r/wordgames/comments/1twp02v/a_question_about_anagrams/
- **Subreddit:** r/wordgames (self-promo: loose)
- **OP question:** "If you put the letters in a circle, like boggle, I see the nine-letter word almost instantly — but in a line (Scrabble) I'm hopeless. Why?"
- **Suggested reply (copy-paste ready):**

There's actual research on this — radial letter arrangements activate visuospatial processing in a way that linear arrays don't. Your brain stops treating it as a "reading task" and starts treating it as a "shape detection task." You're not unusual; tournament Scrabble players train specifically to override this by mentally rotating letter strings, which is genuinely unnatural. The circular format is the honest version of the skill.
