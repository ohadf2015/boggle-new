## What shipped
Rewrote 3 description fields (meta, OG, Twitter) on Spanish scrabble landing page.

**Root cause:** "sin turnos / no por turnos" in all 3 descriptions actively discouraged clicks from searchers wanting "scrabble online" — scrabble IS turn-based, so saying "NOT turn-based" was anti-intent. CTR was 0.2-0.7% at pos 5-7 (expected 4-6%).

**Fix:** Replaced with scrabble-semantic framing: "forma palabras, gana puntos, compite en tiempo real" — intent-aligned without confusing the product positioning.

**Opportunity size:** 9 queries totaling ~800Δ potential monthly clicks all map to this single page.
