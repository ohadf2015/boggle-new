## Friction points
- **Two stranded refs, ~14 nights of failed silent retries.** Every log since 08-03/08-06 prints
  `preflight: WARN — could not auto-recover refs/nightly-pending/2026-08-0{3,6} (cherry-pick/push failed);
  will retry next run`. It has never once succeeded and never escalates. Exactly the Class-4 shape the repo's
  own pitfalls doc names.
- **Lane 11 is burning a slot a night on a blocked target.** Wheel-rush ×6, same verdict every time
  (needs live visual QA + HE/JA native review). The "escalate, don't re-audit" rule was written last week and
  ignored twice since — because nothing enforces it.
- **The authored set reddens the gate on ~3 of 4 gating nights.** Recovery tiers mask it, but each red costs
  40–90 min of gate wall-clock, and nobody knows whether it's lint, test or build because no machine-readable
  per-check record is emitted.
- **Every run-log line is written twice** (verified on 08-17: `lane=01-triage rc=0` and every `▶ Bash` line
  duplicated verbatim). 2.1 MB logs for a 1.5 h night; doubles the cost of every log grep a lane does.
- **The scheduler has no exemption list.** It skipped lane 08 two nights running, which is the lane that owns
  the founder's every-2-days blog directive. A directive-owning lane should not be skippable on idleness.
- **Lane 07 skipped itself on 08-15** as a "zero-signal lane". The lane that measures the loop is the one lane
  whose skip is invisible.

   auto-recover failures per ref; at ≥3 send the Telegram alert instead of a WARN line, and stop retrying
   silently. Effort: S. Impact: closes a 14-night silent failure; two nights of shipped work (08-03, 08-06)
   currently unreachable on master. File: `scripts/nightly/run.sh` (preflight auto-recover block).

   blocker string appears twice, you MAY NOT spend the night auditing that mode's code. Fix the blocker or

   has produced no shippable delta for 6 nights. File: lane 11 prompt.
3. **Emit one machine-readable gate record per night** — `{"date","tier","check":"lint|test|build","rc",
   "authored_red":bool,"outcome":"ship|salvage|rescued"}` appended to `docs/nightly/gate-log.ndjson`.
   Effort: S. Impact: lane 07 can attribute gate reds instead of inferring from 2 MB of prose; would tell us
   in one week whether the recurring red is lint-only. File: `scripts/nightly/lib/gate-isolated.sh`.

   Impact: halves log size and every lane's log-grep cost. File: `scripts/nightly/run.sh`.
