## Friction points
- **The loop cannot ship a fix to itself.** `scripts/` is classified as code, so the docs-only salvage reverts
  it. Lane 7's 10-23 `build:i18n` fix was lost on the night it was written, and three later nights ran
  believing it had landed. This is the single structural defect in the loop. Affects: every lane, permanently.
- **`build:i18n` is still missing from all four tsc chains** (`gate-isolated.sh:142,160,173,203`), so
  `app/[locale]/layout.tsx` throws TS2307 on `messagesManifest.json` in every clean-HEAD worktree. 61 TS2307
  lines across 6 logs. `layout.tsx` is authored by no lane → unattributable → drop-all. Affects: all lanes.
- **A fix was "verified" by a grep that could not match.** `rg 'TS2307 .*messagesManifest'` has a space where
  the real output has a colon. Zero hits read as green. Affects: lane 7's own verification discipline.
- **The peel loop re-runs an identical error set up to 61 times** without noticing it is not shrinking.
  ~5 hours of wall-clock wasted this window. Affects: the gate, every night.
- **A zero-code ship is reported as success.** `isolated-shipped <sha>` printed on 10-23/24/25 for commits
  that `git show --stat` shows are 100% docs. This is why #1 survived two windows undetected.
- **`docs/nightly/reports/` is gitignored** (`.gitignore:82`) — the 7 reports this lane reasons from exist
  only on this machine, while lane prompts call them the report of record.
