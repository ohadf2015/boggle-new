## Friction points

1. **One lane per night stalls ~7.9 h with no output, then exits rc 75 and is reverted — 3/3 nights.**
   It is positional, not lane-specific. **rc 75 is the exit, not the cause:** the 09-10 log holds exactly one
   backoff event, reading `USAGE-LIMIT hit (BACKOFF) — wait 120s would resume past 06:30; aborting (rc=75)`
   — the 06:30 deadline guard firing instantly on an already-dead lane. A 7.9 h `sleeping 120s` loop would
   have left ~230 lines. The stall's mechanism is **unproven**: lane 3 on 09-10 logged ONE tool call across
   the whole span (an `Explore` subagent dispatch), which matches the known `machine sleep kills subagents
   silently` failure — and `caffeinate -i` (`run.sh:34`) holds only an idle-sleep assertion. Nights that
   should end at 03:00 end at 10:30.
2. **The usage window may also be depleted at launch.** On 09-10 lanes 1+2 spent a combined 18 min before
   lane 3 died; the 5-hour pool is shared with the founder's daytime sessions. Currently unmeasured, so it
   cannot be distinguished from friction #1's stall.
3. **`kept N authored file(s)` is the only real output signal, and nothing consumes it.** Lane rc reads
   "continuing" for both a shipped lane and a usage-killed one.
4. **Lane 08 has launched 0/3** while the founder directive asks for a blog post every 2 days. The scheduler
   scores it zero-signal and rotates it out, so the directive is structurally unreachable.
5. **agent-browser still cannot dismiss the cookie-consent overlay** — 25+ nights. Blocks lane 11 visual QA
   and lane 02 CLS capture simultaneously.
6. **Lane 7 was skipped on 09-09**, so this file's predecessor was 6 days stale and led with a headline
   (`_gate_ensure_bin`) that had already been fixed.

## Concrete improvements (ranked by impact × effort)

1. **Add a per-lane WALL-CLOCK CAP (~25–30 min): kill, revert that lane, advance.** Chosen deliberately over
   "bound the usage-limit retry" because it recovers the lane under *either* mechanism — hung subagent or
   usage block — and the mechanism is not yet proven. Note `rg "exit 75|EX_TEMPFAIL" scripts/nightly/run.sh`
   returns nothing: run.sh only *classifies* rc 75 at `:445`/`:512`, the CLI emits it, so there is no in-lane
   wait loop to cap. Every lane after the dead one demonstrably succeeds, so failing fast costs nothing.
   Effort: **S/M**. Impact: recovers ~1 lane of output per night (~12%) and ~7.9 h wall clock; nights finish
   before 04:00. File: `scripts/nightly/run.sh` lane-dispatch block.
2. **Probe and log the remaining usage window at preflight, and timestamp every backoff.** One line —
   `preflight: usage window ~N% remaining` — plus per-event backoff timestamps would have settled improvement
   #1's mechanism tonight instead of leaving it inferred. Effort: **S**. Impact: turns a starved night from
   "a lane looks broken" into a known condition. File: `scripts/nightly/run.sh` preflight block.
   *(Related: if sleep is confirmed, upgrade `caffeinate -i` at `run.sh:34` to `-s`/`-d` — `-i` holds only an
   idle-sleep assertion and does not survive a lid close.)*
3. **Emit `files_shipped=<n>` per lane after the gate.** `kept N authored file(s)` already exists pre-gate;
   promote it to a post-gate, greppable, per-lane line. Effort: **S**. Impact: the loop's health metric stops
   being `rc`, which is blind; lane 7 stops reconstructing the run-health table by hand every week.
   File: `scripts/nightly/run.sh` post-gate block.
4. **Add a union merge driver for the three append-only nightly artifacts.** `docs/nightly/impact-ledger.ndjson`,
   `docs/nightly/mode-readiness.md`, `docs/nightly/perf-baseline.json` conflict on every isolated-ship retry,
   which is why `refs/nightly-pending/2026-08-03` has failed ~38 nights running. Effort: **M**. Impact: three
   stranded refs land; the whole conflict class disappears. File: `.gitattributes`.
5. **Give lane 08 a scheduler exemption or drop the blog directive.** 0/3 launches against a "post every
   2 days" standing order is a contradiction the loop cannot resolve on its own. Either add lane 08 to a
   never-skip list or the founder retires the cadence. Effort: **S** (flag) / founder call.
   File: `scripts/nightly/run.sh:421` scheduler block.
6. **Add a "verify carried items before re-carrying" step to the lane 7 prompt.** Two defects (dup log lines,
   silent ref retry) were fixed but re-reported as open, and the #1 headline was stale by a week. Require a
   one-command verification per carried watch, and mark it CLOSED with the evidence. Effort: **S**.
   Impact: learnings.md stops accumulating phantom debt. File: `scripts/nightly/prompts/07-self-learn.md`.
7. **Pre-seed the cookie-consent cookie before agent-browser's first navigation.** 25+ nights blocking two
   lanes. Effort: **M**. Impact: unblocks lane 11 visual QA and lane 02 CLS capture at once.
   File: the agent-browser bootstrap used by lanes 02/11.
