  nights.** Fails `layout.tsx` (authored by no lane) → `unattributable gate failure` → `docs-only salvage`.

  the per-call tier (full lint+test+build) is the real cost.
- **One lane's volume discards all others.** Lane 01's 37 files sink lanes 02–06's 9 combined. `file-cap=8` is
  printed in the time-guard line but never enforced on the keep path.
- **A zero-code night is reported as success.** 7 commits of proof. `isolated-shipped <sha>` printed on 11-05
  for a 13-doc commit.
- **`docs/nightly/reports/` is gitignored** (`.gitignore:82`) while 7 lane prompts call it their report of
  record. Those files exist only on this machine.
- **Inbound Telegram is dead** — 40 ndjson files, 112 lines, last entry 2026-07-26 (103 days). Seventeenth
