# Competitor + viral concepts — 2026-07-26

## Concepts worth borrowing

- **Instinct Star (speed-play prestige signal)** — source: https://playonemoreletter.com/ (r/wordgames post 2026-07-26)
  - Each round, if a player submits a valid word within ~3s, they earn an "instinct" star displayed on the result. Simple conditional bonus + flash animation. No clue shown, no extra state.
  - Why it works: makes fast skilled play visibly different from methodical. Creates a second leaderboard axis (stars, not just score) that skilled players optimize for and brag about. NYT Wordle's "genius in 2 guesses" is the same mechanic in a different wrapper.
  - Fit: Blast mode — first valid word after a tile wave drops gets the instinct badge. Or Sealed-bid: bidder who submits before the 10s mark gets a lightning bolt in the result card.
  - Effort: S (conditional CSS class + bonus value, one line of state)
  - Risk: low — purely additive

- **Teacher word-list upload → instant mini-games** — source: https://spellingjoy.com/best-apps/spellingcity-shut-down-alternatives (VocabularySpellingCity → Vocabulary A-Z price 3.4× jump, teachers actively fleeing)
  - Teachers paste/upload their own spelling/vocabulary word list; the platform generates playable activities from those exact words. Sticky because teachers invested the list; students play with curriculum-exact words.
  - Why it works: transforms a generic game into a curriculum tool. Teachers don't just use it — they *own* it. Once embedded in a lesson plan, churn is near-zero.
  - Fit: LexiClash Education module. Teacher dashboard already exists at `/teacher`. Add one text-area ("paste your word list") → word-wheel or word-blast seeded from teacher words. The daily drill already supports custom word sets internally.
  - Effort: M (backend word-list store per teacher + game-seed param plumbing)
  - Risk: low — additive, no core game change; education module is already behind a teacher gate

- **Chess-Elo rank display in lobby** — source: SpellHuntr (r/wordgames/comments/1v5f3yv/, 2026-07-26)
  - Competitive word game battler uses chess-style Elo ranking visible in the lobby. Players see their rank *before* the game starts, creating a status anchor that drives requeue.
  - Why it works: pre-game rank visibility is a commitment device — "I'm 1342 Elo, I need to climb." Post-game rank delta (±23) is the variable reward. Same as chess.com's core loop.
  - Fit: LexiClash MP lobby / sealed-bid. The ranked-mode infrastructure (scores, leaderboard) exists. Surface the player's cumulative game score or wins as an "Elo badge" in the versus banner. Lane 11 / sealed-bid: add rank badge to `SealedBidVersus` header.
  - Effort: S (compute a simple rating from existing win/loss data; display in UI)
  - Risk: low — doesn't change game logic; visible stat can be reset/hidden if it feels wrong

- **Pirate season / cosmetic fresh-start reset** — source: Gimkit Season 2 March 2026 (https://triviamaker.com/blooket-vs-gimkit-vs-kahoot/)
  - Every 2-3 months a new themed season resets ranks to Level 1, unlocks 20+ seasonal cosmetics, refreshes the item shop weekly. Players who lost interest re-engage to "start fresh" and hunt exclusive cosmetics.
  - Why it works: fresh-start psychology. The anxiety of being behind disappears; everyone's equal at season launch. Exclusive cosmetics create urgency.
  - Fit: LexiClash MP or Quick Play — quarterly "Season" banner with a new color theme, fresh leaderboard, and one limited avatar/frame per season. No gameplay change.
  - Effort: M (season metadata + cosmetic flag + leaderboard snapshot)
  - Risk: medium — needs founder decision on season cadence; don't ship coins or economy implications autonomously

- **Bilingual synonym quality via embedding filter** — source: r/wordgames/comments/1v5crdv/ (developer post, 2026-07-25)
  - Developer building a bilingual (EN/ES) word game is using definition embeddings to filter false synonym pairs at scale (12k+ pairs). Key failure mode: Linnaean binomials and taxonomic Latin sneaking into datasets.
  - Why it works: dictionary quality is the invisible moat. Players can't articulate why one game's words feel "right" and another's feel cheap — but they feel it.
  - Fit: LexiClash dictionary improvement pipeline. The ES and JA dictionary lanes have both flagged word-quality issues. Embedding-based filtering is a higher-signal filter than frequency cutoff alone. Lane 10 fodder.
  - Effort: L (embedding pipeline), but the insight is S-effort (add a Linnaean/all-caps filter to the candidate scrubber)
  - Risk: low — offline pipeline, no live game change

## What other word-game sites do well

- **SpellHuntr**: Chess-Elo ranking in lobby + daily challenge + private lobbies. Creates a "status at a glance" pre-game moment that turns casual players into rank-climbers.
- **One More Letter** (playonemoreletter.com): Instinct star for pre-clue solutions, 45s per round. The star is the entire social-share mechanic — players post their star count.
- **Blooket** (classroom): 27 named game modes, seasonal exclusives, teacher-assigned homework mode. Surpassed Kahoot by giving teachers *and* students separate win conditions.
- **VocabularySpellingCity → Vocabulary A-Z**: Price tripled overnight. Teachers who depended on it are actively Googling alternatives right now. That is a paid ad we didn't buy.
