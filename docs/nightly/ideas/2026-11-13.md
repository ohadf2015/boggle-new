# Competitor + viral concepts — 2026-11-13

## Concepts worth borrowing
- **Tug (rope-pull word duel)** — source: https://www.reddit.com/r/wordgames/comments/1xtwwsn/i_made_a_word_game_where_you_tugofwar_a_ladybug/ (playexactly.com)
  - 60-second duel vs a bot ("Dot the ladybug"): 12 shared letters, every valid word pulls a rope your way, rarer letters (Z, Q) pull harder than common ones.
  - Works because the reward is visual and instant (the rope moves NOW, not a running score number) and rarity-weighting makes short rare-letter words feel as satisfying as long common ones.
  - Fit: a tug-of-war tension meter for an admin-beta team mode (Wheel Rush / MP) instead of a flat scoreboard — see polish card below.
  - Effort: S (one animated meter component + a weight-by-letter-rarity scoring tweak, letters already have per-language frequency data).
  - Risk: low — purely visual/scoring layer, no new systems.

- **Gimkit's missed-question resurfacing** — source: https://www.msn.com/en-us/news/other/blooket-surpasses-kahoot-in-2026-as-gimkit-and-kahoot-launch-major-updates/gm-GM1B337E83
  - Gimkit explicitly re-serves a question the student got wrong later in the session instead of letting them click past it; Blooket does not, and reviewers cite this as the retention-driving difference between the two.
  - Works because forgetting curves punish "see it once, move on" — one resurfaced miss roughly doubles short-term recall vs none.
  - Fit: Brain Drill (admin-gated, 0 traffic per nightly memory) is a drill mode with no spaced-repetition — resurface each session's missed words 1-2 drills later instead of discarding them.
  - Effort: S — Brain Drill already tracks per-session misses for scoring; queue-and-reinsert is a small state-machine change, no new data model.
  - Risk: low.

- **Gramlet (claim-letter grid, early claims shape later words)** — source: https://www.reddit.com/r/wordgames/comments/1xubv5g/i_made_a_daily_word_game_where_you_uncover_a/ (gramlet.co.uk)
  - Daily 8x8 grid, only the edge visible at start; each turn you claim a letter adjacent to ones you already own, which both reveals new letters AND is the pool you must spell your next word from. Same grid for everyone.
  - Works because early moves have delayed payoff (you're investing in a letter pool, not just scoring immediately) — a soft commitment mechanic.
  - Fit: interesting for Word Tower's "daily letter supply" idea already requested by the founder (06-03 feedback, unshipped) — note only, not a new pitch (mechanic family overlaps what's already in the backlog for that mode).
  - Effort: M. Risk: medium (full board-state rework) — flag for human queue, not a nightly-sized change.

- **Wordle-variant portfolio strategy (Netflix Puzzled, Merriam-Webster/Quordle acquisition)** — source: WebSearch "viral wordle clone 2026" (crosswordle.com roundup)
  - Big players are bundling many small daily word/logic games under one shared-streak umbrella (Netflix Puzzled: 8 games, 1 streak) rather than shipping one deep game.
  - Fit: LexiClash already has this shape (Daily Word Wheel + Word Hunt Survival + Daily Buzz under one streak) — no action needed, just confirms the current daily-hub bet is the right industry direction.
  - Effort: N/A (validation, not a new idea).
