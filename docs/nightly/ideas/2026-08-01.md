# Competitor + viral concepts — 2026-08-01

## Concepts worth borrowing
- **Gimkit "Accuracy Mode" (2026)** — source: https://triviamaker.com/gimkit-alternatives/
  - Removes the speed-timer entirely; scoring rewards correctness over reflex, aimed at vocab/date-style content where reflex punishes slower thinkers.
  - Works because it re-opens the game to players who bounce off timed pressure — same audience our timed Brain Drill (`LightningRound.tsx`) currently excludes.
  - Fit: Brain Drill (admin-beta) — an untimed "Accuracy Mode" toggle, no countdown, same word set.
  - Effort: S — reuse existing drill scoring (`app/api/drills/submit/processCompletion.ts`), gate the timer off, not a new content pipeline.
  - Risk: low — additive toggle, doesn't touch the coin/reward economy.

- **Blooket "Gold Quest" reward-roll on correct answer** — source: https://learnclash.com/blog/games-like-blooket
  - Each correct answer triggers a small randomized visual payoff (not the underlying score) before the next question.
  - Works as a variable-reward micro-hit stacked on top of a mechanic players already do — cheap dopamine, no economy change.
  - Fit: Word-Craft (admin-beta) — RunPageClient already tracks a run; a cosmetic reward-roll on each completed word/milestone is additive polish, not a new system (run structure itself already exists, don't re-pitch that part).
  - Effort: S — purely cosmetic overlay on an existing completion event.
  - Risk: low, as long as the "roll" stays cosmetic and never touches coin amounts (hard line).

- **Beyond Words (Scrabble + roguelike, MindFuel Games, April 2026)** — source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beyond_Words_(video_game)
  - Persistent run/upgrade structure layered on classic tile-word mechanics.
  - NOTE: LexiClash's Word-Craft already ships this shape (`RunPageClient.tsx`, `?mode=cards`/`?mode=gems`) — logged for awareness only, NOT a new pitch. Ladder check: verify-before-pitch saved a duplicate.

- **"What makes you stick with a daily word game?" (r/wordgames thread, 2026-07-28)** — source: https://www.reddit.com/r/wordgames/comments/1v9br6u/what_makes_you_stick_with_a_daily_word_game_and/
  - OP (building their own daily word game) lists their own retention hypothesis: single shared daily puzzle, no unlimited replay, under 5 minutes.
  - Relevant to Word Tower's cross-day-carry design (already shipped, memory: `word-tower-cross-day-carry-shipped-2026-07-22`) — external validation the "one daily puzzle, come back tomorrow" shape is the right retention lever, not a build item.

## What other word-game sites do well
- Gimkit/Blooket (classroom space): both differentiate on a *mode menu* rather than one fixed ruleset — same content, several rule wrappers (speed/accuracy/luck/strategy). LexiClash's education pages already message "no player cap, free forever" as the differentiator (verified in `fe-next/public/llms.txt`) — the mode-menu idea (Accuracy Mode above) is the concrete mechanic gap, not the pricing story.
