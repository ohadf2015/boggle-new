# Competitor + viral concepts — 2026-11-23

## Concepts worth borrowing
- **Roguelike modifier-draft on top of a known mechanic** — source: Cursed Words / Beyond Words (2026 indie releases, both "Balatro/roguelike meets Scrabble")
  - Both fuse a familiar word-game core with a run-based card draft: before each round you pick one small modifier (a joker-style twist) that reshapes scoring or constraints for that round only.
  - Why it works: turns a single-session game into a build-your-run loop — the "what do I draft next" decision is the retention hook, not the word-finding itself.
  - Fit for LexiClash: **sealed-bid** (admin-gated) — before each bidding round, players draft one modifier card (e.g. "blind second bid", "double stakes", "reveal one rival's prior bid") that changes that round's rules. Layers a fresh decision point onto the existing bid mechanic without touching its core.
  - Effort: M. Risk: low — cosmetic/rules modifier only, no economy change.

- **Push-your-luck banking/investment loop** — source: Gimkit's "invest winnings to boost your multiplier, but a wrong answer costs you" compounding mechanic (via WebSearch on 2026 classroom-game platforms)
  - What it does: after each correct answer, the player chooses to bank points safely or reinvest them into a rising multiplier, risking a wipe on the next miss.
  - Why it works: converts a flat scoring stream into a tension decision every round — classic push-your-luck psychology, distinct from any streak/combo meter already live in our modes.
  - Fit for LexiClash: **wheel-rush** (admin-gated, no standalone route — lives at `/en/multiplayer/`) — after a wheel spin lands a scoring segment, let the player "bank" the points or "push" for a 2nd spin at 2x-or-bust. Pure client-side round decision, no server economy change.
  - Effort: S. Risk: low — must clearly separate from the real coin economy (display-only round multiplier, resets every round, never persisted currency).

- **Collectible/rarity meta-layer** — source: Blooket's "blook" collection (tokens earned in-game buy cosmetic collectibles with a rarity score) vs Gimkit's pure compounding-currency approach (WebSearch, 2026 Blooket vs Gimkit comparisons)
  - Why it works: Blooket's persistent cosmetic collection gives players a reason to return even when they're bad at the quiz content itself — collection progress is a parallel win condition.
  - Fit for LexiClash: lower priority this round (word-craft already has an open "Mystery Score" pitch in the ledger using a related collection-adjacent angle — don't stack a second collectible pitch on the same mode). Flagging for a future night on a mode with no pending collectible/economy idea.
  - Effort: L (needs cosmetic asset pipeline). Risk: medium — must stay strictly cosmetic, never touch the real coin economy per standing hard line.
