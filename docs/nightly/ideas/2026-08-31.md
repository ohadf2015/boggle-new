# Competitor + viral concepts — 2026-08-31

## Concepts worth borrowing
- **Gimkit Season 2 (pirate theme, March 2026)** — source: https://www.msn.com/en-us/news/other/blooket-surpasses-kahoot-in-2026-as-gimkit-and-gimkit-launch-major-updates/gm-GM1B337E83
  - Full seasonal reset: every player restarts at Level 1, Item Shop refreshes weekly, 22 exclusive cosmetics tied to the season.
  - Works because it converts a stale economy into a fresh mastery climb on a cadence — players who "finished" the shop have a reason to come back.
  - Fits LexiClash: `word-craft` (`?mode=cards`/`?mode=gems`) already has a gem/card economy with no reset cadence — a seasonal reset + 1 exclusive unlock gives it the same comeback hook.
  - Effort: M. Risk: none (cosmetic-only, no economy-value change — respects the "never touch the coin economy" hard line).

- **Concepto (daily semantic word game, reddit r/wordgames 2026-08-30)** — source: https://www.reddit.com/r/wordgames/comments/1w2q958/concepto_30_aug_2026/
  - Daily challenge, share card shows guess count + a row of colored emoji squares tracking "closeness" per guess.
  - Works because the share card is legible with zero context — a stranger scrolling a feed instantly reads "11 guesses, mostly cold" without opening the game.
  - LexiClash fit: reference pattern for any future daily-mode share card — keep it to a single emoji row + one number, skip prose.
  - Effort: S. Risk: none — already loosely how our existing share cards read; just a confirmation the format is right, no action needed tonight.

- **Five in Five (daily word game, reddit r/wordgames 2026-08-30)** — source: https://www.reddit.com/r/wordgames/comments/1w2i5d9/five_in_five/
  - 5 words, 5 guesses each, one daily puzzle #, share card shows time + remaining guesses in green squares.
  - Works on pure daily-streak pressure — "Five in Five #207" numbering makes missing a day visible.
  - Fit: reinforces that puzzle numbering + a visible streak counter is table stakes for any daily surface we build.
  - Effort: S. Risk: none.

- **VocabularySpellingCity spaced-repetition (education competitor)** — source: https://spellingjoy.com/best-apps/how-to-use-spelling-games-in-the-classroom , https://edtechimpact.com/products/vocabularyspellingcity/
  - Core retention claim: students need ~12 exposures to a new word, spaced across multiple weeks, to move it to long-term memory — so the platform resurfaces missed/weak words in later sessions instead of dropping them after one pass.
  - Works because it's the actual mechanism behind "why did the lesson stick" — not more content, more *re-exposure* of the same words on a delay.
  - LexiClash fit: `brain-drill` (admin-gated) currently has no cross-session memory — a missed word vanishes after that drill. A spaced-repetition queue (missed word resurfaces 1-2 sessions later) is the same idea our classroom "reteach list" already validates works (memory: `classroom-lesson-never-reached-the-board`), applied to solo drill instead of classroom.
  - Effort: M. Risk: low — additive state only, no economy/scoring change.
