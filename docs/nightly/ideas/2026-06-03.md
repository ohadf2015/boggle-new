# Competitor + viral concepts — 2026-06-03

## Founder directive context
`dailyLetterPool.ts` already exists and is fully implemented (100-letter pool, deterministic per date+language, ad refill +15 letters). `sabotage.ts` also exists (skill-earned wrecking-ball tokens, 3 perfect drops = 1 token, cap 3). The gap is UI wiring — tray exhaustion gate, ad surface triggers, rival-sabotage CTA.

## Concepts worth borrowing

- **Letter Boxed / "Burn the Pool" constraint** — source: nytimes.com/games/letter-boxed
  - Uses all letters around a box; you *must* use every letter at least once to win. The scarcity of the pool creates urgency that infinite trays never achieve.
  - Why it works: players feel the cost of each word choice. No wasted letters = mastery dopamine.
  - LexiClash fit: already architected in `dailyLetterPool.ts`. Add a "letters remaining" HUD counter to Word Tower daily mode — single UI file change.
  - Effort: S (wiring only — module is done)
  - Risk: low (no IP similarity to Letter Boxed — mechanic is ours)

- **Word Play / Letterlike roguelike letter upgrades** — source: store.steampowered.com/app/3424020/Letterlike + gamesradar.com/word-play
  - Indie Steam roguelikes where each word "charges" certain letters into upgraded tiles that score multipliers on the next deal. Between-word persistence creates meta-strategy.
  - Why it works: carry-forward reward loop — each good word makes the next hand more powerful. Trending on Steam 2026.
  - LexiClash fit: in Word Tower, scored words could "prime" 1-2 letters in the next tray deal (higher-frequency deal for those letters). Low-risk single-session progression layer.
  - Effort: M
  - Risk: low (roguelike-lite is distinct from main mechanic)

- **Typeshift "use every letter" completion bonus** — source: apps.apple.com/us/app/typeshift
  - Daily puzzle where the goal is using every available letter at least once; a completion badge fires when you hit 100% letter coverage.
  - Why it works: turns "I scored some words" into a completionist goal with visible progress (letters grayed out as used).
  - LexiClash fit: Word Tower daily mode — mark each pool letter as "used" as words consume it. "All letters used" triggers a celebration beat + bonus XP. Syncs with the existing pool exhaustion logic.
  - Effort: S
  - Risk: none

- **Wordscapes reward-ad daily goal arc** — source: apps.apple.com/us/app/wordscapes-word-game
  - Tiered daily goals (3 stages) each gating a better reward; ads are woven in as "watch to unlock next tier early" not as hard paywalls.
  - Why it works: the goal arc gives players a reason to return mid-day without feeling taxed. Proven revenue model.
  - LexiClash fit: Word Tower daily — a 3-tier goal (reach floor 5 / floor 10 / floor 20) where hitting each tier unlocks a cosmetic reward OR a reward-ad to refill letters early. Pairs directly with the daily letter pool.
  - Effort: M
  - Risk: low; Wordscapes is established, not trademarked mechanic

## What other word-game sites do well
- **Poki / CrazyGames portal top words**: "play free instantly" above the fold + thumbnail shows gameplay not logo — drives trial click-through; LexiClash hub tiles could show animated tile-drop thumbnail (not just a label)
- **Letterlike (Steam)**: shows a live "rune forge" preview during loading — player attention during cold-start instead of blank canvas
