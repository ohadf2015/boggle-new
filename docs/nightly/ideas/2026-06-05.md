# Competitor + viral concepts — 2026-06-05

## Concepts worth borrowing

- **Capture-to-Lock Grid** — source: Letter Trap (lettertrap.com) / r/wordgames post [score high, free this week]
  - What it does: letters you USE in words are "captured" off the board; the goal is to clear/lock zones before opponents do. Makes word-selection strategic (board control > raw score).
  - Why it works: transforms word-scoring into spatial puzzle; each word has double value (vocab + territory). Players who know more words don't always win — positioning does.
  - LexiClash fit: Blast modifier "Board Capture" — tiles used by the FIRST player to score them get locked to that player's color; opponent can't reuse them. Adds a zero-sum layer to existing Blast.
  - Effort: M (modifier on top of existing Blast tile system)
  - Risk: moderate territory-mechanic complexity; needs clear visual feedback

- **Shared Solving Board (Palabro mechanic)** — source: r/wordgames, /u/Top-Confection3455, posted ~1 day ago
  - What it does: all players share ONE board; solved words leave letter-hints that help EVERYONE solve the next word. Cooperative-competitive: your success gifts clues to rivals.
  - Why it works: social dilemma = should I solve fast (help others) or slow (hoard info)? Creates tension without adversarial UI.
  - LexiClash fit: Daily mode twist — 5 clued slots, shared letter-reveal cascade. One daily puzzle everyone attempts. Could live as `daily-cascade` tab alongside word-wheel.
  - Effort: M (new daily puzzle type; shared state)
  - Risk: low — no trademark exposure, clearly inspired by El Rosco / Pasapalabra (Spanish show) which is non-US

- **Semantic Distance Multiplayer (WitRush mechanic)** — source: r/wordgames /u/ecolate + Contexto
  - What it does: guess a hidden word by semantic proximity rank (not letters). Multiplayer: race to get closest. Every guess reveals how "warm" you are.
  - Why it works: lowers vocab barrier — players who feel bad at spelling can win on association; spectators understand progress visually.
  - LexiClash fit: Word Hunt variant "Semantic Hunt" — the target word is hidden; players score by submitting words NEAREST to it in embedding space. Bots already exist, could compete here.
  - Effort: L (requires embedding lookup or precomputed similarity table; infra cost)
  - Risk: L effort means multi-night; semantic similarity data needed

- **Daily Venn Diagram (Venndle mechanic)** — source: r/wordgames /u/Ok-Hornet5238
  - What it does: 3 overlapping category circles; 7 words placed in zones; guess what each circle's category IS (not the words). Center intersection = bonus.
  - Why it works: meta-puzzle layer — you're inferring the RULE, not just the word. Connections-adjacent but spatially richer.
  - LexiClash fit: standalone daily mode `word-venn`; admin-only first. Uses existing connections puzzle data (categories + word sets already in DB). Very S effort to prototype.
  - Effort: S (reuse connections data, new UI layout)
  - Risk: close to NYT Connections in spirit; differentiate with spatial layout + category-guessing twist

- **Roguelike Scroll/Perk Word Game (Lexispell / Word Play Steam)** — source: Steam, r/wordgames
  - What it does: words earn currency; currency buys scrolls that CHANGE RULES mid-run (double vowel score, letter wildcard, tile flip). Roguelike loop = each run different.
  - Why it works: meta-progression + rule mutation = highly replayable; "every word I make is a choice" (run economy vs. vocab performance).
  - LexiClash fit: Blast modifier stack as mini-roguelike — between waves, player picks ONE modifier perk from 3 options (already partially in place via wave modifiers). Adding PLAYER-CHOICE to modifier selection (currently random) transforms it into Lexispell territory.
  - Effort: S (modifier selection UX already has infrastructure; change random→choice)
  - Risk: very low — builds on existing system, no new infra

## What other word-game sites do well

- **Lexagon (lexagon.com):** hex tile grid + swipe to form words + hidden clue phrase that reveals gradually — the "discover the secret phrase" meta-goal keeps players in past their first word. Converts lookers into 10-min sessions.
- **Letter Trap (free no-ads pivot):** removing the paywall and announcing it loudly on r/wordgames drove high organic traction this week. "Free, no ads" is a conversion message, not just a feature.
- **Venndle:** daily puzzle + social-share = same NYT formula but with distinctive visual identity (Venn diagram is INSTANTLY recognizable in share-card screenshots → organic spread).
