# Competitor + viral concepts — 2026-11-03

## Concepts worth borrowing
- **Oredle — stack/dig press-your-luck layer** — source: https://oredle.com/ (via r/wordgames, `1xmechj`, posted today)
  - Daily letter-stacking word game (like Upwords) with a "mining/roguelike" twist: dig deeper for gems/fossils, score climbs, but depth is a risk you choose to keep pushing.
  - Why it works: turns a static daily puzzle into a live push-your-luck decision each round — the tension is "bank now or dig one more layer," which is a different hook than pure speed or accuracy.
  - Fit for LexiClash: sealed-bid's bid-amount step is already a risk decision; layering a visible "go deeper" escalation on it is a near-zero-new-UI way to borrow the mechanic. (See headline idea below.)
  - Effort: S. Risk: low — purely an internal round-flow tweak, no new assets.

- **Wordle VS — synchronous same-puzzle race with partial reveal** — source: https://www.reddit.com/r/wordgames/comments/1xmc5vv/ (today)
  - Up to 4 players race the same word; you can't see opponents' letters, only how many guesses/letters they've found so far — enough to feel the heat without spoiling the answer.
  - Why it works: tension from partial information, not full transparency — classic "ghost runner" feel without giving away the solution.
  - Fit for LexiClash: our MP modes already show live scores; a "partial progress ghost" (opponent's guess count, not their board) could work for Word Hunt or Daily MP without a leaderboard rewrite. Not pitched yet as its own mechanic family — flagging for a future lane-04 pass since tonight's two polish-card slots went elsewhere.
  - Effort: M (needs a lightweight progress-broadcast event). Risk: low.

- **EduWords — ad-free, "why do you come back" framing** — source: https://www.reddit.com/r/wordgames/comments/1xmai81/ (today)
  - Crossword-style word connector pitched explicitly as "no ads" with unlockable chapters; OP's own question to the thread was "what makes you keep coming back — puzzles, progression, or daily streak?"
  - Signal, not a mechanic to copy: on a mode-agnostic level it confirms progression/streak framing is still the top retention lever players name unprompted. Supports keeping streak-safe work (already in the ledger for sealed-bid) as a priority, doesn't need a new pitch.

## Education competitor note (founder priority)
- **Gimkit's in-round currency + mid-round power-up spend** beats Blooket's pure mini-game/collectible loop for engagement among older students (middle/high school), per this week's comparisons — the "earn currency, choose when to spend on a power-up" decision is the retention lever, not the theme skin.
  - Vocabulary.com's own stated differentiator: students' *sense of control* over when/how long to play and which words to drill — not the adaptive algorithm itself — is what they credit for retention.
  - **Acquisition tactic worth stealing:** Kahoot Go / PearDeck / Quizlet all gate the generous free tier at the *teacher* signup (school email, not card), then upsell on class-size/analytics limits — i.e. the free tier is sized to cover one real class, not a token demo. Our `/education/esl-word-games` page already ranks for ESL-vocab-game searches (showed up unprompted in today's WebSearch) — the gap to close is matching that "free tier = one real class, not a crippled demo" framing on `/education/for-schools`, not the landing page copy itself.
  - Concrete steal for LexiClash education module: Brain Drill (admin-beta) could borrow the "spend earned points on a mid-quiz power-up" decision from Gimkit instead of a passive progress bar — ties into the polish card below.
