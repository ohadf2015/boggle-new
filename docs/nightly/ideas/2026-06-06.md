# Competitor + viral concepts — 2026-06-06

## Concepts worth borrowing

- **Hidden Crown Mechanic (Lexagon)** — source: https://lexagon.com (r/wordgames post 2026-06-04)
  - As you swipe hex tiles to form words, each word reveals letters of a hidden clue phrase; the "crown word" (completing the puzzle) can only be guessed after enough of the phrase is visible.
  - Why it works: layers a deduction reward on top of pure word-finding — two win states in one session (words found + phrase decoded). Players report "just one more guess" loop similar to Wordle.
  - LexiClash fit: a "Daily Crown" mode — every daily session hides a 3–5 word phrase; scoring words reveals letters; first to guess the phrase earns a bonus multiplier mid-round. Reuses existing tile engine + word dict. Zero new infra beyond a phrase bank (S–M effort).
  - Effort: S (phrase bank is static JSON; UI is a progress bar + guess modal)
  - Risk: low; no trademark exposure; mechanic is generic deduction-reveal

- **Cascade Dependency Scoring (Palabro)** — source: r/wordgames post 2026-06-03
  - A shared board of 5 clued words in a 2-minute timer; solving one word drops its letters into sibling rows as hints. Every solve accelerates the next.
  - Why it works: creates urgency + compounding momentum — completing the first word feels like lighting a fuse. Strong daily-game retention pattern (players return to beat their cascade speed).
  - LexiClash fit: Word Tower already stacks words; adding "cascade hints" (cleared words drop 1 letter into the row below as a free tile) would reward speed without changing the core mechanic. Effort S, zero new infra.
  - Effort: S
  - Risk: low; additive to existing Word Tower mechanic

- **Semantic Proximity MP (WitRush / Semantix)** — sources: r/wordgames posts 2026-06-03
  - Two indie devs shipped the same week: guess a hidden word by semantic closeness (not letters). Rank = how "meaning-close" your guess is. Multiplayer variant shows opponents' proximity gauges live.
  - Why it works: vocabulary depth stops mattering — fluent-but-not-obscure players can compete with vocabulary specialists. Validated by Contexto's sustained daily numbers; adding MP gives it social stickiness. Two devs shipping the same week = confirmed market appetite.
  - LexiClash fit: a "Meaning Race" daily or MP round — all players get the same target word, they guess semantically; leaderboard ranks by proximity after 3 guesses. Embedding into existing MP lobby is M effort (needs word2vec embeddings, can use a small pre-baked similarity table for the top 10k words).
  - Effort: M (similarity table + new UI component)
  - Risk: medium — needs a trusted embedding source; Hebrew/Japanese semantic models are lower quality

## What other word-game sites do well

- **Lexagon (lexagon.com):** No login, no ads, daily fresh puzzle — frictionless entry converts casual visitors. Crown-word reveal creates a shareable "I got it in N words" card with no extra code.
- **Palabro (shared board):** The "2-minute same-puzzle-for-everyone" constraint makes comparison social — players naturally say "I got 3/5, you?" even without a share button. LexiClash already has daily modes but the shared-board constraint could tighten community identity.
- **Poki/CrazygGames word portals:** Feature games with a visible "daily players" count badge — social proof converts skeptics. Our hub doesn't surface real-time active player counts anywhere.
