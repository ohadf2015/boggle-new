# Competitor + viral concepts — 2026-06-20

## Concepts worth borrowing

- **Capture-the-Clue ownership lock** — source: https://www.reddit.com/r/wordgames/comments/1ua5nwk/im_waiting_on_the_ios_store_for_the_new_full/
  - Diction (dictiongame.com) ships live PvP word-hunt where "whoever finds a word first captures it" — the word tile locks in that player's color immediately, ownership is unambiguous and permanent for the round.
  - Why it works: replaces abstract score increments with territorial control. You SEE what you've claimed. Losing becomes legible ("they took 8, I only took 4") and motivating.
  - LexiClash fit: Crossword MP Race already has two players solving the same grid. Applying capture-lock to clues — first correct answer "owns" that clue slot in your color, shown in the clue list — turns the mode from "score race" into "territory race." The shared grid makes this feel dramatic: watching a clue disappear to the opponent's color is more visceral than watching their number tick up.
  - Effort: S (server already broadcasts first-solver; add colored lock badge to CrosswordClueList)
  - Risk: Nil — additive only, no mechanic change

- **Escalating session timer (escape-room tension)** — source: https://www.reddit.com/r/wordgames/comments/1ua27ko/ + Tiled Words review (trizoza, r/wordgames u8ovzg)
  - Multiple threads this week surface the same frustration: difficulty doesn't escalate WITHIN a session — hard mode just means "always hard." Tiled Words is praised specifically for tension that "refuses to reveal itself until critical mass of bits clicked into place." The Connections OP explicitly says "I want categories that actually get harder" (not just "harder overall").
  - Why it works: the emotional arc of escalating pressure is the whole game. Escape rooms work because each room compounds the stakes. Knowing room 3 has only 60s makes rooms 1 and 2 feel like a countdown.
  - LexiClash fit: Word Vault's escape-room framing is literally built for this. Rooms already exist as progression units. Per-room timer that shrinks by 15s (room 1: 90s → room 2: 75s → room 3: 60s) + a pulsing countdown orb that shifts red as it depletes = the tension arc Word Vault currently lacks.
  - Effort: S (hook into existing per-room lifecycle, extend existing timer component)
  - Risk: Could frustrate low-skill players — keep room-1 generous (90s) and add a "Normal / Escape Artist" difficulty toggle

- **Near-miss precision ghost (during-play)** — source: WordDrop escalation curve analysis + crane feedback signal (polish:try ×2)
  - WordDrop's design doc notes "when players lose, they immediately understand WHY and feel they could do better." Crane lacks this: a bad drop just... happens, with no signal showing how close perfect would have been.
  - Why it works: near-miss is psychologically rewarding even in failure. The ghost lets players feel mastery proximity — "I was THIS close" drives retry behavior more than score alone.
  - LexiClash fit: When a Crane block lands within 2 tile-units of the optimal slot, a translucent ghost silhouette pulses for 0.8s at the target position. A running "close calls: 3" counter feeds a session-end badge "Precision Dancer." Distinct from the Highlight Reel (post-session; ⏳ no verdict) — this fires DURING play.
  - Effort: S (position delta already computed in collision logic; add ghost component + session badge)
  - Risk: May read as visual noise on mobile small screens — gate behind `prefers-reduced-motion` check

- **Directional alphabet proximity clue** — source: TUMBLER (r/wordgames, u6o35w) 
  - TUMBLER gives color + UP/DOWN directional clues ("this letter needs to go higher in the alphabet") instead of Wordle's binary present/absent. Players consistently call it the feature that makes the mode "feel different."
  - Why it works: proximity information makes wrong guesses feel productive. "I learned the letter is between M and S" is a gain, not just a loss.
  - LexiClash fit: Sealed Bid's bidding phase currently gives no feedback between rounds. A "proximity reveal" — after each round, the winning word's letters glow on a mini alphabet rail showing "your bid word was in this zone" — rewards strategic bidding and makes rounds feel connected, not isolated. Mechanic family distinct from Word Forge's Directional Hint (which is mastery-unlockable) — here it's post-round scaffolding, not a paid hint tier.
  - Effort: M (need round-history state in bid results, alphabet-rail display component)
  - Risk: Complicates Sealed Bid's intentionally sparse reveal; prototype before committing

## What other word-game sites do well

- **LINX (playlinx.app)** — "no spam, no ads" positioning is first headline. Converts on trust before mechanics. We should mirror this more aggressively on LexiClash's landing — "free, no account required, no ads" before listing modes.
- **TUMBLER crew leaderboard** — social accountability loop (crew sees everyone's daily score) drives daily return without push notifications. Validates the Sealed Bid Round Table idea in BUILD.
- **Diction "capture" feedback** — ownership visualization (word tile locks in your color) is a stronger feedback signal than a number incrementing. Lesson: make scoring events VISIBLE not just numerical.
- **Connections sub thread** — 5+ upvotes in 1.5 days with no answer yet. Genuine demand for "difficulty that escalates WITHIN a session" = unmet market need we could own.
