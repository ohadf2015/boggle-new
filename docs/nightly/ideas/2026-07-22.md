# Competitor + viral concepts — 2026-07-22

## Concepts worth borrowing

- **Post-game mastery replay (NYT Crossplay "Cross Bot")** — source: https://www.pocketgamer.biz/nyt-games-multiplayer-word-game-crossplay-surpasses-one-million-downloads/
  - Cross Bot reviews every move after the match and shows the highest-scoring word available at each turn, with a clear "you could have played X here" overlay.
  - Works because it satisfies the "what was possible?" itch without slowing down the game. Players return specifically to beat their personal-best replay score — a pure mastery loop.
  - Brain Drill is the natural home: after each drill session, flash the top 3 missed words (one letter fanned-in per second, brief glow) with a small delta score. Gives players a concrete "what to practice" signal.
  - Effort: S (read completed round words vs player words, render 3 missed-word animations at session-end screen)
  - Risk: low — no game logic change, purely additive end-screen widget.

- **Gimkit economic simulation ("earn and spend" mid-session)** — source: https://learnclash.com/blog/blooket-vs-gimkit
  - Gimkit turns a vocab quiz into an economic mini-game: correct answers earn in-game currency, which you spend on power-ups mid-session (freeze opponents, double payout, etc.).
  - Works because each correct word creates a DECISION rather than just a confirmation. Strategic depth from a simple mechanic.
  - Sealed-bid could borrow this: a mid-round "Power Token" pool funded by past correct bids. Spend tokens to see one opponent's word length, or to guarantee uniqueness on your next submission. One power slot per round, free to earn.
  - Effort: M (power token state in sealed-bid reducer, one UI slot in the bid panel)
  - Risk: medium — changes sealed-bid balance. Ship behind a flag first.

- **Visual reveal on word completion (WordPeek)** — source: https://www.reddit.com/r/wordgames/comments/1v2oiym/wordpeek_a_game_i_made_where_a_word_search/
  - WordPeek hides a picture behind a word-search grid; finding each word reveals a tile of the image, so the picture gradually appears as you solve.
  - Works because the hidden image creates a curiosity gap that pulls players to "just one more word." The progress is visible and non-abstract.
  - Crane/Word Tower: each successfully stacked word block fades in a slice of the round's silhouette (a tower, a skyline). The shape only becomes legible when 70%+ words are stacked. Encourages completion over early-exit.
  - Effort: M (composite SVG mask behind the game canvas, reveal step per word)
  - Risk: low — purely visual overlay, no rule change.

- **Anti-ad positioning as acquisition hook** — source: https://www.reddit.com/r/wordgames/comments/1v2gxc3/i_built_a_word_puzzle_game_because_i_got/
  - A new indie game launched on r/wordgames framing itself explicitly as "ad-free because I hate ads." The post got traction purely on that positioning — no standout mechanic mentioned.
  - Works because ad fatigue in word games is HIGH (users explicitly naming it as the genre's #1 frustration). "No ads" is a brand promise, not just a feature.
  - LexiClash angle: the `/education` hub and landing pages don't lead with "no interstitials during class." Adding "teachers: zero pop-ups, zero signups, play immediately" to the hero of `/education/for-schools` could convert the frustrated-teacher segment directly. Zero code cost — copy change only.
  - Effort: XS (copy edit, no code)
  - Risk: none.

- **SpellingCity / Vocabulary A-Z pricing gap** — source: https://spellingjoy.com/best-apps/spellingcity-shut-down-alternatives
  - SpellingCity was rebranded post-acquisition to "Vocabulary A-Z" at $108-125/year per classroom — up 3-4× from its original price. Teachers are actively searching for alternatives.
  - Works as a CAPTURE moment: teachers who just lost access to SpellingCity are in an acute "need a replacement NOW" mindset.
  - LexiClash angle: the `/lexiclash-vs-vocabularyspellingcity` SEO page exists but may not surface in "SpellingCity alternatives 2026" searches. Adding "SpellingCity raised prices? Here's what teachers use instead" as an H2 with schema FAQ markup could capture that traffic.
  - Effort: XS (SEO copy + FAQ markup on existing page)
  - Risk: none.

## What other word-game sites do well

- **NYT Games / Crossplay**: post-match AI review creates mastery loop; in-game chat normalises social play in a word-game context; smart skill-based matchmaking reduces frustration for casual players.
- **Blooket**: 27 modes + visual variety keeps classroom re-engagement high; collectible characters make the platform "the game" not just a quiz tool.
- **Poki / CrazyGames portals**: featuring on a portal page for "word games" drives organic discovery — LexiClash standalone build is already submitted to GD (live). Poki submission is the unlock.
