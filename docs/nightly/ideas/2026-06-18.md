# Competitor + viral concepts — 2026-06-18

## Concepts worth borrowing

- **Alphabet-Direction Feedback (Proximath / Word Peaks / TUMBLER)** — source: https://proximath26.itch.io/proximath/devlog/1554963/proximath + r/wordgames TUMBLER thread
  - Each wrong letter tile shows ↑↓ alphabet-direction arrows (and optionally numeric distance) toward the correct letter, converting vague "wrong" into precise "warmer/colder" signal
  - Why it works: players feel measurably smarter on each failed attempt; failure converts to progress-signal rather than frustration; the arrow gives something to *act on* immediately
  - LexiClash fit: **Word Alchemy** — when a word submission misses the required element pattern, show a brief ↑↓ glow pulse on the mismatched letters indicating which direction the transformation should go. Or in **Word Forge**, a forge-heat gradient (cool-blue → orange → white) as each letter approaches the correct slot
  - Effort: S
  - Risk: low — visual feedback layer only, no dictionary/scoring changes

- **Jigsaw-Snap Completion Reveal (Tiled Words)** — source: https://thinkygames.com/news/solve-jigsaw-puzzles-made-of-crosswords-in-the-fresh-daily-puzzle-game-tiled-words/
  - When the final answer is solved, tiles animate into a jigsaw-snap sequence — each piece "clicks" into a locked position with a satisfying visual/haptic beat before the word reveals
  - Why it works: converts the "solved" moment from an instantaneous flash into a 1-2s ceremony; the *tactile* click satisfies a different reward channel than score popups; review: "refusing to reveal itself until a specific critical mass of bits clicked into place"
  - LexiClash fit: **Word Vault** — vault room completion could snap the solved cipher tiles into a final emblem pattern; distinct from Flawless Room Stamp (stamp on perfect clear) and Room Echo (acoustic pulse)
  - Effort: S (animation only — CSS/Framer keyframe sequence on existing tiles)
  - Risk: low; no gameplay change

- **Crew Async Leaderboard (TUMBLER)** — source: r/wordgames post `1u6o35w`, posted 2026-06-15
  - Players form small persistent "crews" (3-8) who all attempt the same daily puzzle; results aggregate async so you see crew standings at end-of-day without requiring live sync
  - Why it works: social accountability without scheduling friction; FOMO from seeing a crewmate beat your score drives daily return
  - LexiClash fit: Closest match is Sealed Bid (Round Table is already in BUILD status), so this mechanic family is occupied — note here for awareness but do NOT pitch as new mode idea
  - Effort: M (requires server-side crew persistence)
  - Risk: overlaps Sealed Bid Round Table (same mechanic family; ledger: BUILD)

- **Tetris-style word placement (Wordoverflows)** — source: r/wordgames post `1u7f5sw`, posted 2026-06-17
  - Letters fall Tetris-style; player positions them to form valid words before the stack overflows
  - Why it works: spatial + lexical pressure simultaneously; time anxiety drives replayability
  - LexiClash fit: **Word Tower** — tower already has block-stacking physics; falling-letter input layer (vs. tap-to-select) could differentiate a "chaos mode" variant within the admin-gated version
  - Effort: M-L (new input paradigm)
  - Risk: scope too large for one nightly lane; flag as longer-horizon idea

- **Reaction Chain Streak Counter (from Sealed Bid bluff-counter + Shiritori streak learnings)** — synthesis across modes
  - A visible chain counter that increments on consecutive correct transformations/words and resets on miss; personal-best chain triggers a bonus reward
  - Why it works: streak hooks are the highest-validated return mechanic in the feedback ndjson (5/9 polish:try votes cite streak or variable-reward)
  - LexiClash fit: **Word Alchemy** — chain of correct elemental transformations; "⚗️ Chain ×7" counter visible during play
  - Effort: S — `useAlchemyStreak.ts` hook + badge overlay
  - Risk: none; pure additive

## What other word-game sites do well

- **Poki / CrazyGames portals**: feature word games with a single-sentence hook visible before click-through ("6 tries, directional clues, crew leaderboard") — LexiClash hub tiles are description-light; adding mode-specific one-liners on each admin-beta tile could lift explore-rate
- **TUMBLER**: crew creation via shareable URL (not in-app invite flow) — zero friction to form a group; worth noting for eventual Sealed Bid Round Table implementation
- **Tiled Words / Proximath**: both do a "daily streak" counter in the share card with social-pressure framing ("streak: 14") — our share cards for admin-beta modes could add personal streak count to build habitual daily return
