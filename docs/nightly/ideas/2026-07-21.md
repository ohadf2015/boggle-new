# Competitor + viral concepts — 2026-07-21

## Concepts worth borrowing

- **Shrinking-timer sudden-death (Shiritori / competitive word-chain apps)** — source: shiritoriwithfriends.com, shiritorigame.com
  - What it does: each turn the countdown drops by 2 seconds. Round 1 is 30s; by round 10 it's 10s. Fail to play → lose a heart.
  - Why it works: creates escalating dread without changing the core rule. Players who survive later rounds feel genuine mastery, not just luck. The timer arc IS the narrative.
  - How it fits LexiClash: **Shiritori Solo** — the mode is 55% ready; the shrinking-timer path (vs flat 15s) is a one-variable change that dramatically ups tension. Also applicable to Blast "Ghost Round" variant (idea:build pending).
  - Effort: S (one config value + UI countdown pulse animation)
  - Risk: low — existing Shiritori timer framework already there

- **Instinct Bonus (One More Letter — anagram ladder)** — source: reddit.com/r/wordgames/comments/1v0m9ke
  - What it does: if you solve BEFORE a hint appears (first 20 of 45s), you earn an "instinct" star. Hint appears automatically but costs the star.
  - Why it works: converts impatience (wanting the hint) into a meaningful choice — take the star or take the help? Variable-reward mastery hook.
  - How it fits LexiClash: **Sealed Bid** — submitting your word in the first 3s of a round earns an "instinct" chip (visible to opponent). Doesn't change scoring; pure prestige/social signal that makes fast play feel skilled. Low collision with existing auction mechanic.
  - Effort: S-M (timer visible, chip UI, one boolean per round)
  - Risk: low

- **Wrong-word resurface loop (Gimkit spaced repetition)** — source: gimkit.com, learnclash.com/blog/blooket-vs-gimkit
  - What it does: incorrectly answered questions cycle back more frequently until the student gets them right. Acts as in-session spaced repetition.
  - Why it works: mistake = teaching moment, not punishment. 2026 Gimkit data shows measurably better retention vs Kahoot's one-shot per question.
  - How it fits LexiClash: **Education module** — teacher creates a word list; the ESL/spelling-bee practice game resurfaces words the student got wrong more often in the same session. No backend change needed; pure front-end round-queue reordering weighted by error count.
  - Effort: M (round queue weight logic + teacher-dashboard word-list UI if not already present)
  - Risk: low — no scoring/economy change; purely pedagogical

- **WordFlow phrase-chain with auto-reveal as strike (new indie)** — source: reddit.com/r/wordgames/comments/1v0fis9
  - What it does: 8-word chain, first word revealed, every adjacent pair forms a phrase. Every 15s a letter of your stuck word auto-reveals — but each reveal costs a strike. You're racing your own curiosity.
  - Why it works: converts a passive clue system into an active cost/benefit decision. Players feel smart for resisting, and feel fine taking hints because it was their *choice*.
  - How it fits LexiClash: **Daily puzzle** — existing daily Boggle grid could add a "letter reveal" button that costs 50% of the word's score. Reframes hint-use from shame to strategy. Educational angle: teachers can track hint count per student.
  - Effort: M
  - Risk: low

- **Blooket "accuracy over speed" mode (Kahoot 2026 update)** — source: triviamaker.com/blooket-vs-gimkit-vs-kahoot
  - What it does: Kahoot's new Accuracy Mode removes the speed timer. Correct answer = points regardless of when submitted.
  - Why it works: removes accessibility barrier for slower typers/readers. Expands appeal to classroom + older demographics without hurting competitive feel.
  - How it fits LexiClash: **Education / classroom Shiritori** — a teacher-toggled "accuracy mode" where timer shows progress but doesn't punish. Same words, same chain rules, just no timer penalty. Relevant to ESL learners who need thinking time.
  - Effort: S (one toggle per session config, conditional timer display)
  - Risk: low

## What other word-game sites do well

- **One More Letter** (anagram ladder): free, no account, mobile-first — converts in Reddit comments because it's instantly playable without friction. Zero-signup conversion path is the strongest differentiator in the r/wordgames community right now.
- **Shiritori With Friends** (shiritoriwithfriends.com): pace picker (10s/15s/30s) is the lowest-effort personalization that addresses the #1 word-game complaint ("too slow"/"too stressful"). Letting the player choose pace before the game starts reduces abandonment.
- **Gimkit 2026 Season 2**: pirate cosmetic theme + Item Shop refreshes weekly → cosmetic seasonality drives re-engagement without changing core mechanics. Their **season resets** (everyone back to Level 1) are controversial but create fresh competitive cycles.

## Education competitor — key steal

**Gimkit wrong-answer resurfacing** is the education mechanic most worth stealing into LexiClash's `/education/spelling-bee-practice` and `/education/esl-word-games`. Blooket surpassed Kahoot in 2026 usage — the differentiator cited by educators is self-paced + adaptive difficulty. LexiClash has the word quality infra; what it needs is a session-local "error weight" queue so students face their weakest words more. This maps directly onto the teacher dashboard's word-list feature.
