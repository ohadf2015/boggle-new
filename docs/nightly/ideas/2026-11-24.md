## Concepts worth borrowing
- **Live in-round combo/heat visual tied to answer streaks** — source: WebSearch on 2026 Gimkit vs
  Blooket comparisons (Gimkit's currency-and-power-up loop is explicitly tied to consecutive-correct
  answers; teachers cite it as the system's main "feels like progress" signal, distinct from the
  final score).
  - What it does: a visible, escalating indicator (flame/glow) grows while a player keeps scoring
    without a miss, and resets on a miss — pure feedback, no currency.
  - Why it works: rewards skill in the moment, not just at results; keeps weaker players informed
    they're "cooling off" without being told outright.
  - Fit for LexiClash: **wheel-rush** (admin-gated, lives at `/en/multiplayer/`). Checked
    `fe-next/backend/modes/wheelRush.ts` (34 lines) — no multiplier/combo state exists there today,
    and the ledger's only pending wheel-rush pitch is the discrete post-spin "bank or push" decision
    (2026-11-23), which is a different mechanic (one-time choice vs. continuous momentum display).
    `WheelRushView.tsx` already imports a `useQuickReactions`/`FloatingReaction` emoji-reaction system
    (`components/game/QuickReactions.tsx`) — confirmed that's shipped platform-wide, so a "sticker
    reaction" pitch would have been a dupe; picked the heat/combo angle instead.
  - Effort: S — reuse the visual primitive WordCraft's own Heat/overdrive meter already defines rather
    than building a new one; wheel-rush just needs the client-side streak counter.
  - Risk: low — display-only, no economy.

- **Cross-session streak + one streak-freeze token** — source: Duolingo's streak mechanic (WebSearch;
  repeatedly cited as its top loss-aversion retention lever, with a "streak freeze" that forgives one
  missed day).
  - What it does: a counter that grows for each consecutive day a mode is played, resets on a gap,
    with a single freeze that survives one missed day.
  - Why it works: gives a mode a reason to be opened again tomorrow, independent of that session's
    content — a pure return-hook rather than a session-mechanic.
  - Fit for LexiClash: **brain-drill** (admin-gated; routes live under `/brain/drills/<type>`, e.g.
    `lightning-round`). Checked `fe-next/components/brain/DrillPageShell.tsx` and
    `BrainCheckPanel.tsx` — zero `streak` references today. Daily Challenge already has a streak
    (`fe-next/public/llms.txt`), but applying the same proven mechanic to a *different*, currently
    streak-less admin-beta mode is new work per this lane's own rule, and it's a different mechanic
    family from the already-pitched "Twin Drill" (simultaneous dual-board, a single-session layout
    change, not a cross-day counter).
  - Effort: S — localStorage counter + last-completed-date, one freeze flag, a flame+count badge in
    the existing drill shell header. No persisted currency.
  - Risk: low.

- **Co-op "Boss Battle" team-vs-one mode** — source: Gimkit's Boss Battle (all-but-one Challengers
  cooperate against a single Boss, teacher-recommended as the Boss seat).
  - Why it works: asymmetric team structure is a different social dynamic than every other mode we
    have (all FFA or 1v1); strong classroom-fit ("teacher vs class") hook.
  - Fit for LexiClash: deprioritized tonight — every current admin-beta mode (`wheel-rush`, `word-craft`
    Cards/Gems, `brain-drill`) is solo or symmetric-FFA; retrofitting an asymmetric team structure onto
    any of them is a real rules change, not a night-sized polish slice. Flagging for a dedicated-budget
    night, not pitching as tonight's card.
  - Effort: L. Risk: medium (rules change, needs its own balance pass).

## What other word-game sites do well
- **Gimkit / Blooket 2026 comparison articles** (gimkits.co.uk, classcortex.com, etc.) all lead with
  "which wins for YOUR classroom" framing rather than feature lists — they sell the teacher's specific
  use case (fast formative check vs. long review game vs. async homework), not the product. Worth
  noting for how `/education/for-schools` copy pitches itself, not a new mechanic to build.
