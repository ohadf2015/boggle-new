## Full Description (4000 chars max)

```
You know that feeling when you find a word nobody else saw? That rush is what LexiClash is built on.

Swipe letters, build words, outscore your friends — all happening live, not "your turn in 3 hours." Real opponents, real pressure, real satisfaction when you drop a 7-letter bomb while everyone else is stuck on CAT.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

Most word games are slow. You play a word, put your phone down, wait for grandma to respond next Tuesday. LexiClash runs in real time. Everyone plays the same board, same clock, same panic. It's closer to a party game than a crossword — think Jackbox meets Boggle.

4 LANGUAGES, 1 GAME

Play in English, Hebrew, Swedish, or Japanese. Not just translated menus — actual word dictionaries for each language. Switch mid-session if you want. Your Swedish friend and your Japanese coworker can finally stop arguing about who has the better vocabulary.

GAME MODES

Word Arena — Jump into live multiplayer battles. Quick Start finds you an opponent in seconds. Or create a private room and settle things with people you actually know.

Daily Challenge — Same puzzle for everyone, every day. Compare your score against the whole player base. Good for ego. Sometimes bad for ego.

Adventure — 100+ levels across themed worlds (Root Caverns, Synonym Springs, Alphabet Meadows). Each world throws new rules at you. Not just "find words" — you'll deal with gravity, locked tiles, and other curveballs.

Practice — Solo mode. No timer pressure. Just you and the grid. Ideal for warming up or pretending you're being productive.

THE DESIGN

Dark theme, bold colors, thick borders, zero gradients. If you're tired of apps that look like they were designed by the same person, you'll like this. We call it neo-brutalist. You can call it whatever you want.

YOUR PROGRESS

Global leaderboards with tier rankings (Bronze through Diamond). Track your best words, longest streaks, and highest scores. Compete for #1 or just try to beat your roommate.

ACTUALLY FREE

No paywall blocking levels. No "watch 30 ads to continue." Play the full game without spending anything. We have optional cosmetics if you want to look fancy, but the game works fine without them.

Download it. Find a word. Talk trash to your friends about it.
```
(1,797 chars — well within 4,000 limit)
