## GEO / AI Visibility Recommendations

These queries are question-shaped and would benefit from FAQPage schema + direct answer paragraphs (for AI overviews and zero-click answers).

The Spanish page already has **FAQPage**, **VideoGame**, **SoftwareApplication**, **HowTo**, and **BreadcrumbList** schema — good. The boggle page has **FAQPage**, **HowTo**, **WebApplication** — good. The blog page has **FAQPage** + **BlogPosting** — good.

**Priority GEO improvements:**

1. **"word games like boggle"** (blog page) — The FAQPage schema exists but no explicit Q&A about the best alternatives. Draft addition:
   ```
   Q: What are the best word games like Boggle?
   A: The best free word games like Boggle in 2026 are: LexiClash (multiplayer, up to 50 players), Wordle (daily, solo), and Wordscapes (mobile-first). LexiClash most closely replicates Boggle with a letter grid and real-time multiplayer — free, no download.
   ```

2. **"how to play boggle online"** — No dedicated FAQ item on the boggle page. Add:
   ```
   Q: How do you play Boggle online for free?
   A: Go to lexiclash.live, click Play, choose a grid size (4×4 to 6×6), and start finding words. No download or signup needed. For multiplayer, create a room and share the link — up to 50 players can join.
   ```

3. **"scrabble online gratis"** (Spanish page) — The FAQ already exists. Verify it includes a direct answer in the first sentence of the response (Google often truncates to the first 50–60 words).
