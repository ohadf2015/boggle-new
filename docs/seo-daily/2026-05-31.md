## Root cause analysis — Spanish CTR crisis

Queries like `scrabble online` (660 impr, pos 8.0, 0.30% CTR), `scrabble en linea` (525 impr, pos 8.3, 0.19% CTR), `scrabble online español` (560 impr, pos 8.7, 0.00% CTR) all land on `/es/juego-de-palabras-multijugador`.

**Why 0% CTR at positions 7-9?** Competitors at positions 1-7 are turn-based async games (Scrabble GO, Apalabrados/Cruzaletras, Wordfeud). Users choose from those results without scrolling to position 8. Our old meta description mentioned "en tiempo real" but buried it after "sin registro / sin descarga". The key differentiator — **real-time, not turn-by-turn** — was never the FIRST thing a user saw.
