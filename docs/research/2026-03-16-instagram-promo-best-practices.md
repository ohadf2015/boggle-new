# Instagram Promotional Best Practices for Mobile Word Games (2025-2026)

## 1. Post Formats & Dimensions

### Reels (highest priority)
- **Aspect ratio**: 9:16 vertical (1080x1920px)
- **Duration sweet spot**: 7-15 seconds for max retention
- **Character limit**: 72 chars for Reels ad copy
- **Hook window**: First 1-3 seconds must stop the scroll
- **Engagement**: 30-50% more engagement than static posts

### Carousels (second priority)
- **Aspect ratio**: 1:1 (1080x1080px) or 4:5 (1080x1350px, recommended - more screen real estate)
- **Slides**: Up to 10 slides; first slide is the hook, last slide is CTA
- **Use case**: Tutorials, "5 words you didn't know", before/after gameplay

### Single Image
- **Aspect ratio**: 4:5 (1080x1350px) for feed dominance
- **Square**: 1:1 (1080x1080px) still works for grid aesthetics
- **Stories**: 9:16 (1080x1920px)

### General
- 90%+ users are on mobile — always design vertical-first
- Short-form video is the #1 format users interact with for brand content
- UGC-style content consistently outperforms polished studio content

## 2. Copy Formulas That Drive Downloads

### Framework: AIDA
1. **Attention**: Bold claim or question ("Your vocabulary is bigger than you think")
2. **Interest**: Pain point ("Tired of mindless scrolling?")
3. **Desire**: Benefit ("Train your brain in 2-minute word battles")
4. **Action**: Single CTA ("Tap to play free")

### Framework: 4U (for headlines)
- **Useful**: "Build vocabulary while having fun"
- **Urgent**: "Daily challenge resets in 3 hours"
- **Unique**: "The only word game with real-time multiplayer battles"
- **Ultra-specific**: "Found 47 words in 90 seconds"

### Framework: Problem-Solution
- "Bored on your commute? [Problem] Play a 2-min word clash against real players [Solution]"

### Framework: Social Proof + Benefit
- "10,000+ players daily. Your brain will thank you."

### Key Rules
- 125 char limit for most ad types, 72 for Reels
- Single Action Principle: one CTA per post (asking for like+comment+share = getting none)
- 80/20 rule: 80% value content, 20% promotional

## 3. Color Psychology & Visual Hooks

### Colors for Gaming/Word Apps
| Color | Effect | Use For |
|-------|--------|---------|
| **Yellow/Gold** | Energy, optimism, attention-grabbing | Score highlights, rewards, CTAs |
| **Blue** | Trust, calm, intelligence | Brand trust, "brain training" positioning |
| **Green** | Growth, success, correct | Correct answers, streaks, progress |
| **Red/Orange** | Urgency, excitement, competition | Timers, challenges, limited-time events |
| **Purple** | Creativity, premium, mystery | Premium features, achievements |
| **High contrast combos** | Scroll-stopping | Dark bg + neon accents perform best |

### Visual Hook Rules
- Content with strong visual hooks gets 54% higher engagement (Sprout Social 2025)
- Images processed 60,000x faster than text
- Bold typography overlays on gameplay screenshots
- Unexpected camera angles or zoom on satisfying moments
- Color complexity (not just brightness) increases engagement per ScienceDirect research
- Neo-brutalist aesthetic (bold borders, bright fills, playful shadows) is trending and matches LexiClash brand

### Scroll-Stopping Techniques
1. **Pattern interrupt**: Unexpected color/layout vs typical feed
2. **Text overlay**: 3-5 words max, readable at thumbnail size
3. **Face/emotion**: Human reactions to gameplay moments
4. **Motion**: First frame of Reel should have movement/action
5. **Contrast**: Dark background + bright game elements

## 4. CTA Best Practices

### Platform CTAs (for ads)
Best performers for app installs: "Download", "Learn More", "Sign Up"

### Organic Post CTAs
- **Direct**: "Link in bio to play free"
- **Engagement**: "Drop your high score below"
- **Challenge**: "Can you beat 30 words in 90 seconds? Try now"
- **FOMO**: "Today's daily challenge expires at midnight"
- **Social**: "Tag someone who thinks they'd beat you"

### CTA Placement
- Reels: verbal CTA at end + text overlay + caption
- Carousels: last slide dedicated to CTA
- Stories: swipe-up/link sticker + text CTA
- Feed: first line of caption (before "more" truncation)

## 6. What Makes Gaming App Posts Go Viral

### Content Types That Perform
1. **Satisfying gameplay clips**: Chain reactions, high combos, last-second wins
2. **UGC/reaction style**: Screen record + face cam of genuine reactions
3. **Challenge format**: "Can you find X words?" with timer overlay
4. **Before/after**: "Day 1 vs Day 30" vocabulary growth
5. **Behind-the-scenes**: Dev showing how a feature was built
6. **Memes**: Word game humor (finding obscure words, losing to a friend)

### Viral Mechanics
- **Shareability**: Posts people tag friends in ("this is so you")
- **Relatability**: Universal experiences (struggling with a puzzle, last-second clutch)
- **Controversy/debate**: "Is QOPH a real word?" generates comments
- **Series/continuity**: Daily challenge results create return viewers
- **Community challenges**: "Beat my score" with branded hashtag

### Posting Cadence
- 3-5 posts/week minimum
- Best days: Monday-Thursday
- Peak hours: 10am-2pm (audience timezone)
- Respond to all comments (73% will switch to competitor if ignored)
