# LexiClash User Research Protocols

## Overview

This document contains research protocols to validate journey map findings and gather insights for improvement. Includes interview guides, usability testing scripts, survey templates, and analytics tracking plans.

---

## Part 1: User Interview Guides

### 1.1 New Player Interview (Post First Session)

**Objective:** Understand first impressions, friction points, and initial motivations

**Duration:** 20-30 minutes

**Participant Criteria:**
- Played LexiClash for the first time within past 24-48 hours
- Completed at least one full game
- Mix of single/multiplayer first experiences

---

#### Interview Script

**Introduction (2 min)**

> "Thank you for taking the time to chat with us about your experience with LexiClash. There are no right or wrong answers - we want to understand your genuine experience to make the game better. Feel free to be completely honest, even if something didn't work well for you."

**Section A: Discovery & First Impressions (5 min)**

1. "How did you first hear about or find LexiClash?"
   - Follow-up: "What made you decide to try it?"

2. "When you first saw the landing page, what was your initial impression?"
   - Follow-up: "Was anything confusing or unclear?"

3. "What did you think the game was about before you started playing?"
   - Follow-up: "Was it what you expected once you started?"

**Section B: Onboarding Experience (5 min)**

4. "Walk me through what happened when you first started playing. What do you remember?"

5. "Was there a tutorial or introduction? What did you think of it?"
   - Follow-up: "Was anything confusing? Too long? Too short?"

6. "When you were shown how to play, what stuck with you? What did you forget?"

7. "Was there anything you had to figure out on your own that you wish had been explained?"

**Section C: First Game Experience (8 min)**

8. "Tell me about your first actual game. How did it feel?"
   - Prompts: exciting, overwhelming, fun, confusing, stressful

9. "What was the hardest part about your first game?"

10. "Did you understand how scoring worked? How about combos?"

11. "How did you feel when the game ended? Did the results make sense to you?"

12. [If multiplayer] "What was it like waiting for the game to start? How did you join?"

13. [If single player] "What made you choose single player? Would you try multiplayer?"

**Section D: Return Intent (5 min)**

14. "Did you play more than once? Why or why not?"

15. "What would make you want to come back and play again?"

16. "Is there anything that would stop you from playing again?"

17. "Would you recommend this game to a friend? Why or why not?"

**Section E: Wrap-up (3 min)**

18. "If you could change one thing about the game, what would it be?"

19. "Any other thoughts or feedback you'd like to share?"

### 1.2 Active Player Interview (Regular Users)

**Objective:** Understand engagement patterns, feature usage, and retention drivers

**Duration:** 30-40 minutes

**Participant Criteria:**
- Played 10+ games total
- Active in past 7 days
- Mix of game modes and engagement levels

---

#### Interview Script

**Introduction (2 min)**

> "Thanks for being part of our player community. We want to understand what keeps you playing and how we can make LexiClash even better. Your feedback directly shapes the game."

**Section A: Play Patterns (5 min)**

1. "How long have you been playing LexiClash? How often do you play?"

2. "Walk me through a typical session. When do you play? For how long?"

3. "Which game mode do you play most? Why that one?"

**Section B: Engagement Features (10 min)**

4. "Tell me about your achievements. Which ones are you proud of? Which are you working toward?"
   - Follow-up: "How do you know what achievements are available?"

5. "Do you pay attention to your level and XP? What does leveling up mean to you?"

6. "Do you play the daily challenge? Tell me about your experience with it."
   - Follow-up: [If yes] "How important is your streak to you? Have you ever lost one?"
   - Follow-up: [If no] "What would make you try it?"

7. "Have you looked at the leaderboards? What did you think?"

**Section C: Social & Multiplayer (8 min)**

8. "Have you played with friends? How did you invite them?"
   - Follow-up: "What worked well? What was frustrating?"

9. "Have you ever created a room for others to join? What was that like?"

10. "Have you ever shared a win or achievement? Where did you share it?"

11. "What would make you more likely to invite friends to play?"

**Section D: Pain Points & Wishes (10 min)**

12. "What's the most frustrating thing about playing LexiClash?"

13. "Has anything ever made you want to stop playing? What kept you?"

14. "Is there a feature or mode you wish existed?"

15. "If you were designing the game, what would you do differently?"

**Section E: Competitive & Social Dynamics (5 min)**

16. "How competitive do you feel when playing? Does this change based on who you're playing with?"

17. "Have you ever felt the game was unfair? Tell me about that."

18. "What's the most satisfying moment you've had playing LexiClash?"

### 1.3 Churned Player Interview (Lapsed Users)

**Objective:** Understand why players stopped playing and what might bring them back

**Duration:** 15-20 minutes

**Participant Criteria:**
- Played 3+ games historically
- Hasn't played in 30+ days
- Willing to discuss experience

---

#### Interview Script

**Introduction (2 min)**

> "We noticed you played LexiClash a while back but haven't been on recently. We're trying to understand what leads players to take a break and what might bring them back. No pressure - we just want to learn."

**Section A: Play History (3 min)**

1. "Tell me about when you used to play LexiClash. How often? What mode?"

2. "What did you enjoy most about the game?"

**Section B: Departure Reasons (7 min)**

3. "When did you stop playing? Do you remember what led to that?"
   - Prompts: Got busy, lost interest, frustration, found another game

4. "Was there a specific moment or experience that made you stop?"

5. "Did anything about the game frustrate you or feel unfair?"

6. "Did you feel like you were making progress? Why or why not?"

**Section C: Return Potential (5 min)**

7. "Have you thought about coming back? What's holding you back?"

8. "What would make you want to play again?"
   - Prompts: New features, friends playing, rewards, simpler experience

9. "If we fixed [issue they mentioned], would that change your mind?"

**Section D: Alternatives (3 min)**

10. "Are you playing other word games or casual games now? Which ones?"
    - Follow-up: "What do they do better? What do they do worse?"

## Part 2: Usability Testing Scripts

### 2.1 New User Onboarding Test

**Objective:** Observe how new users navigate from landing page to completing first game

**Duration:** 30-45 minutes

**Equipment:**
- Screen recording software
- Think-aloud protocol
- Task checklist
- Emotion tracking sheet

**Participant Criteria:**
- Never played LexiClash
- Familiar with mobile/web games
- Comfortable thinking aloud

---

#### Test Protocol

**Setup (5 min)**

> "Today we're going to test a word game. I'll ask you to complete some tasks while thinking aloud - telling us what you're thinking, what you're looking at, and how you're feeling. There are no wrong answers - we're testing the game, not you."

**Warm-up (3 min)**

> "First, tell me: do you play word games? Which ones? What do you like about them?"

---

**Task 1: Landing Page Exploration (5 min)**

> "I'm going to show you a game. Look at this page and tell me what you think it is, what you can do here, and what you'd click first."

Observe:
- [ ] First area of focus
- [ ] Time to understand purpose
- [ ] Confusion points
- [ ] Stated intentions

Questions after:
- "What do you think this game is about?"
- "What options are available to you?"
- "Which would you choose and why?"

---

**Task 2: Complete Onboarding (8 min)**

> "Go ahead and start playing however you'd like."

Observe:
- [ ] Path taken (single/multi)
- [ ] Tutorial engagement level
- [ ] Skip behavior
- [ ] Confusion moments
- [ ] Time to complete

Questions after:
- "What did you learn from that introduction?"
- "Was anything confusing?"
- "Was it too long, too short, or just right?"

---

**Task 3: Play First Game (10 min)**

> "Play a full game and let me know what you're thinking throughout."

Observe during gameplay:
- [ ] Word formation attempts (successful/failed)
- [ ] UI element discovery
- [ ] Emotional reactions
- [ ] Feature discovery (combos, timer, score)
- [ ] Verbalized confusion

Observe at results:
- [ ] Understanding of score
- [ ] Reaction to achievements
- [ ] Interest in replay

---

**Task 4: Specific Actions (5 min)**

> "Now I have some specific things I'd like you to try."

a) "Find out what achievements you can earn."
b) "Start a new game with different settings."
c) "Find out how to play with a friend."

Observe:
- [ ] Navigation path
- [ ] Time to complete
- [ ] Errors made
- [ ] Frustration level

---

**Wrap-up Questions (5 min)**

1. "On a scale of 1-10, how easy was it to get started?"
2. "What was the best part of the experience?"
3. "What was the worst or most confusing part?"
4. "Would you play this game again? Why?"
5. "What would you change?"

### 2.2 Multiplayer Flow Test

**Objective:** Evaluate the room creation, joining, and sharing experience

**Duration:** 25-30 minutes (per participant, ideally paired testing)

**Setup:** Two participants or facilitator as second player

---

#### Test Protocol

**Task 1: Create and Share Room (10 min)**

> "Pretend you want to play with a friend who isn't here. Create a game room and show me how you'd invite them."

Observe:
- [ ] Room creation completion rate
- [ ] Sharing method chosen
- [ ] Time to generate invite
- [ ] Confusion points
- [ ] Information shared (code, link, QR)

Questions:
- "How would your friend know how to join?"
- "Was anything unclear about this process?"
- "What would make this easier?"

---

**Task 2: Join Existing Room (8 min)**

> "Your friend sent you this code/link. Join their game."

Provide: Room code or join link

Observe:
- [ ] Time to join
- [ ] Method attempted (code vs browse)
- [ ] Errors encountered
- [ ] Profile setup friction

---

**Task 3: Waiting Room Experience (7 min)**

> "You're now waiting for the game to start. Tell me what you see and how you feel."

Observe:
- [ ] Actions taken while waiting
- [ ] Engagement with available features
- [ ] Time perception comments
- [ ] Abandonment thoughts

Questions:
- "How long would you wait before giving up?"
- "What would you want to do while waiting?"

---

### 2.3 Daily Challenge Flow Test

**Objective:** Evaluate the daily challenge experience including streak mechanics

**Duration:** 20 minutes

---

#### Test Protocol

**Task 1: Find and Start Daily Challenge (5 min)**

> "Find and play today's daily puzzle."

Observe:
- [ ] Navigation path
- [ ] Understanding of one-attempt rule
- [ ] Streak awareness
- [ ] Pre-game anxiety

---

**Task 2: Post-Challenge Evaluation (5 min)**

After game completion:
- "What happens now?"
- "How did you do compared to others?"
- "When can you play again?"
- "What does your streak mean?"

---

**Task 3: Streak Recovery Scenario (5 min)**

> "Imagine you missed yesterday's puzzle. Show me what you'd expect to see today."

Discuss:
- Expectations for missed day
- Feelings about lost streak
- Interest in recovery options

## Part 4: Analytics Tracking Plan

### 4.1 Key Events to Track

#### Discovery Stage
| Event | Properties | Purpose |
|-------|------------|---------|
| `page_view` | page, source, utm_params | Track entry points |
| `landing_cta_click` | cta_type (single/multi/how-to) | Measure intent |
| `language_changed` | from, to | Localization usage |

#### Onboarding Stage
| Event | Properties | Purpose |
|-------|------------|---------|
| `onboarding_started` | source | Start rate |
| `onboarding_step_viewed` | step_number, step_name | Funnel analysis |
| `onboarding_step_skipped` | step_number | Skip patterns |
| `onboarding_completed` | duration_sec, skipped_steps | Completion rate |
| `onboarding_abandoned` | last_step, duration | Drop-off analysis |

#### Gameplay Stage
| Event | Properties | Purpose |
|-------|------------|---------|
| `game_started` | mode, difficulty, player_count | Mode preferences |
| `word_submitted` | word, valid, length, points | Gameplay patterns |
| `combo_achieved` | combo_level, duration | Feature usage |
| `game_completed` | mode, score, rank, duration | Completion rate |
| `game_abandoned` | mode, reason, time_remaining | Abandon patterns |

#### Engagement Stage
| Event | Properties | Purpose |
|-------|------------|---------|
| `achievement_unlocked` | achievement_id, name | Achievement popularity |
| `level_up` | new_level, xp_total | Progression tracking |
| `leaderboard_viewed` | leaderboard_type | Feature engagement |
| `profile_viewed` | own_profile | Self-monitoring |

#### Retention Stage
| Event | Properties | Purpose |
|-------|------------|---------|
| `daily_challenge_started` | puzzle_number, streak_count | Daily engagement |
| `streak_milestone` | streak_length | Motivation tracking |
| `streak_broken` | previous_length, days_inactive | Churn risk |
| `session_started` | days_since_last, session_number | Return patterns |

#### Social Stage
| Event | Properties | Purpose |
|-------|------------|---------|
| `room_created` | room_code | Creation rate |
| `room_joined` | join_method (code/link/qr/browse) | Join patterns |
| `share_initiated` | share_type, context | Share intent |
| `share_completed` | share_type, platform | Share success |
| `referral_converted` | referrer_id | Viral coefficient |

### 4.2 Funnel Definitions

**Funnel 1: New User Activation**
```
1. Landing page view
2. Game mode selected
3. Onboarding started
4. Onboarding completed
5. First game started
6. First game completed
7. Second session started
```

**Funnel 2: Multiplayer Completion**
```
1. Multiplayer selected
2. Create/Join chosen
3. Room created/joined
4. Waiting room entered
5. Game started
6. Game completed
```

**Funnel 3: Daily Challenge Habit**
```
1. Daily challenge page viewed
2. Challenge started
3. Challenge completed
4. Day 2 return
5. Day 7 return
6. Day 30 return
```

**Funnel 4: Share to Join**
```
1. Share button clicked
2. Share method selected
3. Share completed
4. Link clicked (attributed)
5. Referred user lands
6. Referred user plays
```

### 4.3 Key Metrics Dashboard

| Metric | Formula | Target | Frequency |
|--------|---------|--------|-----------|
| **Activation Rate** | First game completers / Landing views | >30% | Daily |
| **D1 Retention** | Day 1 returners / Day 0 players | >40% | Daily |
| **D7 Retention** | Day 7 returners / Day 0 players | >20% | Weekly |
| **D30 Retention** | Day 30 returners / Day 0 players | >10% | Monthly |
| **Avg Session Length** | Total game time / Sessions | >8 min | Daily |
| **Games per Session** | Total games / Sessions | >1.5 | Daily |
| **Daily Challenge Rate** | Daily completers / DAU | >40% | Daily |
| **Share Rate** | Shares / Game completions | >5% | Weekly |
| **Viral Coefficient** | Referred joins / Shares | >0.3 | Weekly |
| **NPS** | Promoters - Detractors | >40 | Monthly |

| Churned player interviews | Monthly | 3-5 | 1.5 hours total |
