# Viral Mechanics Reference

Comprehensive guide to designing features that spread organically.

## The Viral Equation

**K-factor = i × c**
- i = invitations per user
- c = conversion rate of invitations

**Goal**: K > 1 means exponential growth

### Improving Invitations (i)
- Reduce friction to share
- Create natural share moments
- Make sharing feel valuable to sharer
- Provide multiple share channels

### Improving Conversion (c)
- Make shared content compelling
- Lower barrier to try
- Provide instant value
- Create curiosity

## Share Trigger Framework

### When Players Share

1. **Achievement moments**
   - Personal best score
   - Rare achievement unlocked
   - Level milestone reached
   - Streak continuation

2. **Social proof moments**
   - Beating a friend
   - Topping a leaderboard
   - Finding rare word
   - Perfect game

3. **Emotional peaks**
   - Clutch comeback
   - Hilarious fail
   - Unexpected victory
   - Close competition

4. **Completion moments**
   - Daily puzzle solved
   - Challenge completed
   - Tournament finish
   - Season end rank

### Share Content Quality

**Good share content**:
- Tells a story
- Creates curiosity
- Shows personality
- Invites response
- Non-spoiling for puzzles

**Wordle's format success**:
```
Wordle 543 4/6

⬛🟨⬛⬛⬛
⬛⬛🟩🟩⬛
🟩⬛🟩🟩⬛
🟩🟩🟩🟩🟩
```

**Why it works**:
- Visual pattern recognition
- Shows journey without spoiling
- Compact and shareable
- Invites comparison

## Network Effect Strategies

### Direct Network Effects
Value increases as more friends join

**Implementation ideas**:
1. **Friend leaderboards**: Compare only with friends
2. **Direct challenges**: "Beat my score on this board"
3. **Collaborative mode**: Team word-finding
4. **Group streaks**: Keep streak alive with friend group

### Indirect Network Effects
Content/features improve with more users

**Implementation ideas**:
1. **Community word voting**: More voters = better validation
2. **Global word statistics**: See how rare your finds are
3. **User-generated puzzles**: More players = more content
4. **Replay highlights**: Best moments from community

## Social Proof Elements

### Leaderboard Design
- Show player's rank prominently
- Highlight "players like you" nearby
- Celebrate movement up/down
- Show friend rankings prominently

### Activity Indicators
- "X friends played today"
- "X players online now"
- "X puzzles solved worldwide"
- Real-time activity feed

### Validation Systems
- Display verified achievements
- Show playtime/games played
- Highlight rare accomplishments
- Seasonal badges and titles

## FOMO (Fear of Missing Out) Mechanics

### Healthy FOMO (Motivating)
- Daily puzzles (miss today, miss forever)
- Limited-time events
- Seasonal themes
- Streak milestones

### Implementation Guidelines
- Don't punish too harshly for missing
- Allow recovery mechanisms
- Focus on reward, not penalty
- Be transparent about timing

### Anti-patterns to Avoid
- Aggressive push notifications
- Loss of significant progress
- Pay-to-recover mechanics
- Manipulative countdown timers

## Invitation Loop Design

### Frictionless Invitation
1. One-tap share from result screen
2. Auto-generated message (editable)
3. Deep link to specific content
4. No login required to view

### Invitation Incentives
**For inviter**:
- Cosmetic reward
- Bonus XP
- Achievement unlock
- Exclusive content

**For invitee**:
- Immediate playability
- Bonus starting resources
- Special "welcomed by friend" badge
- Tutorial skip option

### Conversion Optimization
- Show friend's name/avatar
- Display friend's achievement
- Create curiosity about result
- Make first game satisfying

## Viral Coefficient Benchmarks

### Industry Standards
- K < 0.5: Low virality, needs paid growth
- K 0.5-0.9: Moderate virality, sustainable
- K 0.9-1.0: Near-viral, efficient growth
- K > 1.0: Viral, exponential potential

### Word Game Estimates
- Wordle: ~0.8-1.2 (at peak)
- Words With Friends: ~0.4-0.6
- Scrabble GO: ~0.3-0.5

### LexiClash Targets
- Current estimate: ~0.3-0.4
- Target with improvements: ~0.6-0.8
- Viral event potential: ~1.0+

## Viral Event Design

### What Makes Events Viral

1. **Synchronicity**: Everyone plays at same time
2. **Stakes**: Something meaningful to win
3. **Shareability**: Easy to discuss and share
4. **Spectacle**: Visual/exciting to watch
5. **Accessibility**: Anyone can participate

### Event Ideas for LexiClash

**"World Word War"**
- Global real-time tournament
- Region vs region
- Live leaderboard
- Scheduled event time
- Spectator mode

**"Mystery Letter Monday"**
- Special letter with unknown bonus
- Community solves together
- Shared discovery moment
- Social discussion encouraged

**"Speed Demon Challenge"**
- 1-minute ultra-fast mode
- Weekly leaderboard reset
- Top times shared
- Replay feature

## Measurement Framework

### Key Viral Metrics
1. **Share rate**: % of sessions with share
2. **Share conversion**: % of clicks that play
3. **Invite conversion**: % of invites accepted
4. **Viral cycle time**: Days from share to reshare

### A/B Testing Ideas
- Share button placement
- Share message templates
- Incentive structures
- Social proof elements

### Success Indicators
- Organic traffic growth
- User-to-user referrals
- Social media mentions
- Community growth rate
