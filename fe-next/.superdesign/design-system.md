# LexiClash Design System

## Style: Neo-Brutalist Dark Gaming
- Dark backgrounds (neo-navy #1a1a2e)
- Hard shadows (NO blur, NO soft shadows)
- Bold 3px borders with black
- High contrast neon colors on dark
- Playful, game-like feel inspired by Jackbox Party Pack
- Rounded corners (8-24px)

## Colors
Primary: #BFFF00 (lime), #00FFFF (cyan), #FF1493 (pink), #8B5CF6 (purple)
Background: #1a1a2e (navy), slate-800/80 (cards)
Text: white (headings), gray-300/400 (secondary)
Accents: #FDE047 (yellow badges)

## Typography
Display: Fredoka — bold, rounded, playful
Body: Rubik — clean, readable

## Component Style
- Cards: rounded-3xl, 3px colored border, hard colored shadow, slate-800/80 bg
- Buttons: rounded-neo, 3px black border, hard shadow, press animation
- Badges: small rounded pills with bold text
- Icons: Lucide React, 16-20px
- Labels: 10px uppercase tracking-wide gray-400

## Spacing
Cards: p-4 (compact) / p-6 (full)
Grid gaps: gap-3 to gap-4
Section margins: mb-4

## RTL
Full RTL support for Hebrew — use logical properties
