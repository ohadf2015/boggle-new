## Quality Checklist (For Each Article)

Before considering an article "humanized":

### Content Checks
- [ ] No listicle format ("10 Ways", "5 Secrets")
- [ ] No generic openings ("Think X is Y? Think again")
- [ ] No future dates ("Recent studies from 2024-2025")
- [ ] Personal anecdote in opening
- [ ] At least 2 casual asides in parentheses
- [ ] At least 2 questions to reader
- [ ] Specific examples with numbers
- [ ] Honest confession or admission

### Language Checks (Per Language)
- [ ] Hebrew: Sounds Israeli (colloquial phrases)
- [ ] English: Uses contractions and casual tone
- [ ] Swedish: Natural Swedish (not formal)
- [ ] Japanese: Natural Japanese (not textbook)
- [ ] Spanish: Informal tú, warm tone

### Technical Checks
- [ ] React component structure unchanged
- [ ] All props and types match
- [ ] Images still load
- [ ] Links still work
- [ ] RTL works for Hebrew
