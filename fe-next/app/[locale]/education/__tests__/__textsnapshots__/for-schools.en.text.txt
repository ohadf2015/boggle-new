LexiClash is a word-building game (not multiple-choice), supports six languages including Hebrew RTL, fits a whole class of up to 50 students for free, and offers true 1v1 duels.Can I use LexiClash for ESL or English language learner (ELL) students?Yes — LexiClash was built with language learners in mind. It supports English, Hebrew (RTL), Spanish, Swedish, Japanese and Russian with native dictionaries. Students can compete in their own language or practice the language they are learning, making it a natural fit for ESL, bilingual and immersion classrooms. Word-building gameplay reinforces vocabulary and spelling organically, without passive multiple-choice.Can students use LexiClash as a spelling practice game?Yes. LexiClash's word-building format — where students find, spell and submit real words against a live opponent or the whole class — makes spelling practice competitive and engaging rather than passive.
