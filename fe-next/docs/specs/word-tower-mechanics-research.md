# Word Tower — addictive-mechanics research (shortlist)

**Date:** 2026-05-23 · Online research, ranked by impact ÷ effort, ethical patterns only.

## Top 3 to build next
1. **Daily Seed Tower** (S) — everyone climbs the SAME deterministic Shiritori grid each
   day; comparable scores, daily-return habit, kills RNG-unfairness complaints.
   (NYT Spelling Bee model.)
2. **Combo multiplier with loss-aversion reset** (S) — unbroken chain ramps 1×→2×→4×; one
   invalid word resets to 1× (not zero). Drives "one more word" recovery. Transparent.
3. **Milestone "tease" banners** (S) — at 85–95% of the next zone show "Next: Aurora /
   Asteroid field". Near-miss + escalation pull. Landmarks already exist; just wire the tease.

## AVOID (predatory)
Energy/lives timers · loot-box reward chests · paid mid-run revives · watch-ad-to-keep-combo.
Word games retain on **skill + habit**, not time-gates or variable-ratio paywalls.
