# Pyramid daily art (2026-11-17)
Model: Higgsfield nano_banana_2 (2k), reference image public/daily/modes/connections.webp (mascot house style).
Sprites came from one 2x2 contact sheet on a flat magenta background, keyed to alpha, trimmed, resized and saved as webp q82.
- stone-block.webp: sandstone brick, front view, blank face (tiers are rows of these, carved with the bridge word in CSS).
- capstone.webp: golden pyramid capstone with sparkles (set on top when the pyramid is won).
- explorer.webp: the cube mascot in a safari pith helmet, cheering (stands on the highest laid tier).
- dust-puff.webp: sand dust cloud (puffs out when a tier lands).
- desert-dusk.webp: 21:9 backdrop, stepped dusk sky bands, setting sun, dunes, two distant pyramids. Generated separately, no keying.
