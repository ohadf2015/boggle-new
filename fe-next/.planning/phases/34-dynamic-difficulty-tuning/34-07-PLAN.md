```typescript
  startSession: startAIDirector,
  endSession: endAIDirector,

  level: config.level,
```

```typescript

```

4. **End AI Director session (in level complete/fail handling):**
Find where LevelCompleteModal or defeat handling occurs and add:
```typescript

```

5. **Record words on submission (in word validation handler):**
Find the word submission handler (likely `handleWordSubmit` or similar) and add after word validation:
```typescript

```

6. **Handle transitions at combo breaks:**
Find where combo resets (when an invalid word is submitted that breaks combo) and add:
```typescript

```

7. **Handle transitions at power-up activations:**
Find power-up activation handlers and add:

8. **Wire intensity adjustments to hint system:**

