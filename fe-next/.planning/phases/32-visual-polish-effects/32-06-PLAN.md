1. Start dev server: `npm run dev`
2. Open http://localhost:3000

**POLISH-01: Victory Confetti**
1. Complete a level successfully (reach objectives before time runs out)
2. Observe: Level complete modal should show confetti burst
3. Expected: Neo-brutalist confetti (yellow, pink, cyan squares) fires on modal open

**POLISH-02: Boss Defeat Fireworks**
1. Navigate to a boss level (typically levels 5, 10, 15, 20 in each world)
2. Defeat the boss (reduce HP to zero)
3. Observe: Fireworks animation should appear
4. Expected: 6-15 firework bursts depending on boss tier (mini/standard/elite)

**POLISH-03: Combo Milestone Celebration**
1. During gameplay, chain words quickly to build combo

**POLISH-04: Layered Particle Effects**
1. Trigger a 10+ combo celebration
2. Observe particle layers:

   - Mid-ground: Medium burst particles (pink/cyan/red)
   - Foreground: Fast sparkles (white/yellow)
3. Expected: Clear depth perception with 3 distinct layers

**POLISH-05: Cinematics (Optional - may be in Phase 33)**
1. Complete a level to trigger victory cinematic
2. Lose a level (time out) to trigger defeat cinematic
3. Expected: 5-6 second Remotion animations with skip button after 2s

**POLISH-06: Particle Budget**

2. Trigger multiple celebrations
3. Check FPS counter during particle effects
4. Expected: Consistent 60fps, no jank

**Accessibility Test:**
1. Enable reduced motion in OS settings:
   - macOS: System Settings > Accessibility > Display > Reduce motion
