## Key Competitors Identified

1. **Scrabble GO** - Traditional Scrabble gameplay, heavy ads
2. **Words With Friends** - Scrabble-like, social focus
3. **Ruzzle** - Fast-paced 2-minute gameplay
4. **Boggle With Friends** - Direct Boggle alternative
5. **Word Battle** - Real-time PvP duels
6. **Last Word** - Battle royale format

## Market Insights

- Mobile gaming market: $135B (2025) → $229B (2030)
- Puzzle games: ~20% of all mobile game downloads
- Word games growing in market share
- Asia-Pacific leads with 54% revenue share
- In-app purchases: 61% of revenue
- Ad revenue growing fastest at 15% CAGR
